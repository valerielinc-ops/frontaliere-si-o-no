**THESIS** — Make the rules, scenarios and consequences easy to compare so a cross-border worker can identify which situation applies and what to do next. Refuse the one-size-fits-all article stack.

**OWN-WORLD** — Extend the existing Frontaliere Ticino identity: white space, navy editorial type, purple action color, Space Grotesk headings and Inter body copy. Use documentary photography from the border region and precise, restrained comparison tables; do not change the site-wide visual identity.

**STORY** — Readers arrive with a question, locate the rule or scenario that matches their case, understand the difference, then open the relevant calculator, map or next guide. The signature interaction is opening a scenario row to inspect its full simulation; hover and focus feedback stay brief, and motion never hides content. Keep every existing factual claim, value, legal source and calculation intact.
