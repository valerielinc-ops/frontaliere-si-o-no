Role overview

Position Purpose Senior Scientist with high level of experience in protein biophysics to characterize a variety of therapeutic proteins.

The incumbent applies a strong technical background in biomolecules characterization to exciting interdisciplinary projects working in a collaborative team environment on state-of-the-art instrumentations.

He/she will partner with other scientists and external partners to develop fit-for-purpose analytical assays, suggest workflows and provide technical support and training to enable critical process decision-making and support continuous drug development activities.
