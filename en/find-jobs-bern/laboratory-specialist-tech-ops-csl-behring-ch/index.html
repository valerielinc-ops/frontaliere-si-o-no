Role overview

Job Summary The Laboratory Specialist, Tech Ops supports day‑to‑day laboratory operations and technical activities that enable development, characterization, optimization, and lifecycle management of therapeutic products and associated processes.

The role performs routine and moderately complex laboratory tasks, generates high‑quality data, and ensures that all work is conducted in alignment with established procedures, quality standards, and regulatory expectations.

The incumbent applies foundational scientific knowledge and practical laboratory skills to contribute to experimental execution, equipment operation, data documentation, and troubleshooting under guidance from more senior colleagues.
