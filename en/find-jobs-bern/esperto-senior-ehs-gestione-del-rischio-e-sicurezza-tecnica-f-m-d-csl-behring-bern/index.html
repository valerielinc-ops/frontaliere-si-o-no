Role overview

Responsible as Senior EHS Expert – Risk Management & Technical Safety (f/m/d) They develop, implement and continuously improve a systematic, ISO‐45001-compliant EHS risk management at the Bern site.

The role includes the moderation and implementation of risk analyses, risk assessments, pSIF investigations, technical safety assessments as well as the intensive EHS supervision of construction, plant and process projects.

In addition, the role of the EHS specialist coordinates: inside projects and acts as a technical hub between engineering, operations, quality and EHS.
