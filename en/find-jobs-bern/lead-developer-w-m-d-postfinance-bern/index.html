Role overview

At PostFinance we develop high-quality applications that come with modern technologies and high quality as quickly as possible with our customers.

Our test automation team provides the right tools to ensure efficient test processes from level system.

As a lead developer (w/m/d), you not only actively shape the end-to-end test automation solution, but also guide the team in technical decisions, promote best practices and drive innovation – with expertise, team spirit and strategic view.

We meet twice a month in Bern and additionally twice a month at another location (Zofingen, Olten or Zurich).
