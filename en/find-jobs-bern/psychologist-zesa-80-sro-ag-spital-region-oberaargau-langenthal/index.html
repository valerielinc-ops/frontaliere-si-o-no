Role overview

We are an interdisciplinary team of experts from the fields of medicine, psychology, nutrition and diabetes counseling as well as physiotherapy, who work according to an interdisciplinary therapy concept with continuous and individually adapted medical, psychological, exercise and sports therapy as well as nutritional support.

The ZESA is an outpatient clinic for internal medicine.

It is important for applicants in training for a specialist title to know that for this reason we unfortunately cannot offer a psychiatric-psychological year, which is required in order to be able to bill independently in the arrangement model.
