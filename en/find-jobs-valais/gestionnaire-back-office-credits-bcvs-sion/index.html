Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities will lead you to process, monitor and manage credit requests for the clients of individuals and businesses.
  • You will actively contribute to the proper maintenance of client records (from a time-scale point of view-quality-compliance with regulations) through the irreproachable processing of data and take appropriate action in case of potential or proven risks.
  • You will collaborate on the tasks of ICS (systems of internal controls) and contribute to compliance with the credit policy.
  • Finally, you will support customer advisors and encourage constructive exchanges to achieve win-win results.
