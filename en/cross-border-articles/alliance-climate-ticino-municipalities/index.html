Let's look at some of the most relevant initiatives. ### Adopt sustainable lifestyles Reducing energy and water consumption is an important step towards a more sustainable lifestyle. Cross-border workers can adopt simple habits, such as turning off lights when not needed, using energy-efficient appliances, and installing efficient heating and cooling systems. Additionally, they can reduce water consumption by adopting practices such as rainwater harvesting and installing water-saving devices. ### Use sustainable means of transport Using sustainable means of transport is another way to contribute to the fight against climate change. Cross-border workers can opt for public, shared, or electric transport to travel between their workplace and residence. Additionally, they can participate in carpooling programs or use bicycles for short trips. ### Participate in local environmental volunteering initiatives Participating in local environmental volunteering initiatives is a concrete way to contribute to the sustainability of the territory. Cross-border workers can join local groups that organize cleaning activities, tree planting, or environmental awareness campaigns. Additionally, they can attend events and conferences to stay updated on the latest news regarding climate change and sustainability. ### Monitor local policies Monitoring local policies on climate change is another way to actively contribute to the fight against climate change. What are the main measures adopted by the alliance between the Canton and the municipalities to address climate change?
The main measures adopted by the alliance between the Canton and the municipalities include promoting sustainable mobility, improving the energy efficiency of buildings, and waste management.
