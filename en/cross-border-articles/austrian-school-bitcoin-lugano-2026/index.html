Frequently Asked Questions
Who are the main economists of the Austrian School?
The main economists of the Austrian School include Ludwig von Mises, Friedrich Hayek, and Murray Rothbard. These economists have significantly contributed to the development of economic theories that still influence economic and financial thought today.
How can I start investing in Bitcoin?
To start investing in Bitcoin, it is advisable to read 'The Bitcoin Standard' by Saifedean Ammous for an introductory overview. Afterwards, you can delve deeper into the works of Austrian economists and participate in events and conferences dedicated to Bitcoin and Austrian economics.
What are the implications of the Austrian School for investments in cryptocurrencies?
The Austrian School, with its focus on methodological individualism and the subjective theory of value, offers a unique perspective that can be applied to the analysis of cryptocurrencies. Understanding these theories can help make more informed investment decisions tailored to your specific needs.
