Description The School of Life Sciences (SV) Sustainability Office is responsible for environmental sustainability within this faculty in EPFL.

Working closely with the Dean’s office, we collaborate with the scientific, administrative, and student community to implement the “Green Lab” vision.

This vision promotes environmentally and socially responsible research without compromising scientific excellence.
