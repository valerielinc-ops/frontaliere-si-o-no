Entry as a train driver for passenger traffic 12/2026 Chur, 01.12.2026, 100% Apply now ## What you can look forward to. As a train driver, you do great things: At FFS Passenger Traffic, you move around 482 million passengers every year. After completing the paid 15-month training, the job takes you along the most beautiful railway lines in Switzerland. In doing so, you bring our passengers to their destination on time and ensure a positive travel experience with FFS. Your everyday work is also full of perspectives with attractive working conditions for all life situations. Night and weekend work are additionally compensated - for example, with 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. During night shifts, you also have time supplements between 10 and 30 percent. This means you have more free time than employees without night and weekend shifts. In addition, after training as a train driver, you can design your individual working hours from 60 percent, with different part-time models available. Learn more about the professional image at the next information event and visit us at the next driver's cab visit. ## What you can do. - As a train driver, you not only take the lead of the train, but also take responsibility for safety and punctuality on the densely used rail network. - During your journey, you are attentive and react quickly and correctly to signals, speed regulations and unpredictable events. - You act independently and are always in contact with operational centers and control rooms. - In addition to driving and maneuvering trains, checking the technical condition before and after taking over the vehicle is part of your tasks. ## What you bring with you. - You have completed a three-year apprenticeship or maturity. Your motivation is more important to us than the industry you come from. - You approach challenges calmly, make considered decisions even in stressful situation
