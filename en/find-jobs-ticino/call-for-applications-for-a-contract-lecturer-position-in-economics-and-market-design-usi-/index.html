  • The Università della Svizzera italiana (USI) is a young and dynamic institution, continuously evolving and embracing new challenges, guided by its three core values: quality, openness, and responsibility.
  • Active across a wide range of study and research areas, USI offers its students an engaging, interdisciplinary learning experience that encourages initiative, while providing its researchers and staff with a stimulating environment characterized by autonomy and freedom of initiative.
  • The Faculty of Economics at USI stands out for its international and interdisciplinary approach, aimed at training economists who are well equipped to operate competently in a global context.
  • Given that Lugano is the third largest financial centre in Switzerland, the Faculty places particular emphasis on teaching and research in finance, management, and economics more broadly.
  • One of its key strengths is the close interaction between students and faculty members: the teaching system is designed to foster continuous engagement and individual attention.
  • The bachelor’s program offers specializations in finance, quantitative methods, management, and political economy.
