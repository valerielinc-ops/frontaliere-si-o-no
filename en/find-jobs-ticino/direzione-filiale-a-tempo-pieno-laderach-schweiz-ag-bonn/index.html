Original description

Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach is known for its high-quality fresh chocolate. Founded in 1962, the family-owned business today employs over 2500 people in over 80 countries, with a growing trend. The company's headquarters is located in the Canton of Glarus. In a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates and develop new and innovative ideas in agile teams. The "Retail" department comprises over 250 chocolate shops in over 30 countries. Here, we offer our chocolate specialties and invite our customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Do you also want to be part of it? Then come to us. You can expect Total responsibility for the results of the sales location, while adhering to the predetermined budget Leadership, management, and motivation of the store and its employees Ensuring quality standards Ensuring and adhering to internal store guidelines Communication with the company's headquarters Active participation in the store and in sales. You bring Team leadership and motivation: You should be able to lead a team, motivate it, and, if necessary, coach it to achieve common goals. Open communication: You should be able to communicate clearly and openly, both with your team and with customers and the company's headquarters. Time management: You should be able to manage multiple tasks simultaneously, such as personnel planning, inventory management, and sales strategies, and maintain a clear overview. Sales strategies: You should have a good sense of sales promotion and know how to present products attractively to increase sales Experience in retail, ideally in a leadership position, will help you understand and apply the various aspects of business operations better. What we offer An exciting work environment with motivated colleagues A position with a lot of personal responsibility The opportunity to introduce new ideas An open and respectful feedback culture Internal and external training Individual career opportunities Generous
