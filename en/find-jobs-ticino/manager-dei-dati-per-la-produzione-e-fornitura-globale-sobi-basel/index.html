Role overview

As a Data Manager within Global Manufacturing and Supply (GMS) you will be the key person to build and manage the processes, governance and system landscape for our data.

In other words, you shape the vision of our future data management set up and will be the driver of the data journey within GMS.

In our view, good data needs to enable correct and on time supplies to patients and create positive business impact.
