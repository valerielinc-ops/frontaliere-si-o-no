Original description

Ready for a groundbreaking adventure in the track sector? In this role, you're not just driving one of our modern two-way baggers, but you're also directly involved in track construction. Whether you're behind the wheel of the bagger or out on the job site, your assignments are varied, responsible, and technically demanding. Here's what we expect from you: - Operation of our Liebherr A922 Rail including all attachments - Independent small maintenance of the bagger and equipment - Execution of bagger work, transports, and interventions in the track sector - Active participation in track construction - you're flexible and not just as a machine operator Here's what we expect from you: - Training as a machine operator, at least category M1, M2 preferred - Basic training on two-way baggers VTE10 or willingness to complete it - Experience in track construction or construction industry preferred - Driver's license category B (or category F up to 45 km/h) - Stamina, independence, and high flexibility - Willingness to work nights and weekends - Good German language skills Your benefits - what you can look forward to: - We promote a flexible planning of your tours and shifts - You receive a GA 2. Class, a GA 1. Class, or a half-fare ticket. - You have free time when others are working. For meals at night and on weekends, there are supplements. - It is possible to buy five to ten additional vacation days per year. - Financial support for external training and an interesting internal learning and course offer are at your disposal. - With seasonal events and courses, we support your health About BLS With almost 4000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. And much more: We are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company, and we safely transport cars through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What unites us? The pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.
