Original description

Are you an independent, customer-oriented sales professional looking for an innovative and flexible work environment? Then you're exactly right with us! Hügli Nahrungsmittel GmbH is a leading manufacturer of high-quality food products and offers innovative solutions for the food industry. Our company philosophy is based on quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction. Join our committed team and contribute your sales expertise to the further expansion of our market presence in the region around Düsseldorf - Krefeld - Aachen. Tasks: Competent point of contact and expert consulting for your customers (gastronomy, hotels, large kitchens and canteens) on our product portfolio. Identification and acquisition of new customers to expand our customer base. Maintenance and development of existing customer relationships through regular visits and proactive care. Implementation of sales strategies and actions to increase the sales of our products. Creation of daily reports and sales reporting, as well as independent tour planning. Requirements: Completed training as a chef or in the food sector, ideally supplemented by commercial knowledge. Professional experience in the field, preferably in the food industry. Developed sales and negotiation skills. High motivation and goal orientation. Strong communication skills and persuasion skills. Secure handling of MS Office, especially Excel and PowerPoint. Travel readiness and driving license class B.
