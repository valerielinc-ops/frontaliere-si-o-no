Main responsibilities

  • Define and document core motion control concepts (architecture, control strategies, safety principles) to be applied across projects
  • Design and implement motion control algorithms for electric motors and robotic systems
  • Perform system commissioning and tuning, including PID parameter optimization and motor calibration
  • Collaborate with Hardware and Electrical teams to ensure seamless integration of control software with drives, sensors and actuators
  • Document software architecture, control strategies, and commissioning procedures for internal and external stakeholders
  • Ensure training and technical support of the company’s engineers

  • Expertise in control theory (PID, feedforward, cascaded loops)
  • Familiarity with motion control hardware (drives, encoders, sensors)
