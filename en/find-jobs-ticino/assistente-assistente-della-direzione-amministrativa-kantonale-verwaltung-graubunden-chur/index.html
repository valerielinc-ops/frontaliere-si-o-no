Description The Department of Economy and Tourism currently employs 20 staff members. In your role as Assistant to the Department Head, you will support as a member of the secretariat team the Department Head and staff in various administrative, organizational, and coordination tasks. With your commitment, you contribute to an efficient and smooth operation within the Department. Do you have a strong service understanding? Do you maintain clarity even in hectic times? Then you are exactly right with us. Strengthen our motivated team by 1st August 2026 or upon agreement as Assistant to the Department Head and staff member of the Secretariat (80-100%). ## Tasks - Proactive support of the Department Head in day-to-day operations and projects - Hub between internal units and external partners - Appointment coordination and preparation before and after meetings - Drafting and editing of correspondence and protocols - Responsibility for and participation in administrative tasks of the Secretariat - Substitution in Creditor Management - Training and supervision of apprentices - First point of contact for IT matters within the Department ## Requirements - Basic commercial training and an advanced qualification in a relevant field (e.g. HF/FH with a focus on Executive Assistance, Public Administration or comparable) - Initiative, reliable and solution-oriented personality with experience in an assistant function in a demanding and diverse environment - Very good written and oral communication skills in German and knowledge of another cantonal language and English - Friendly and professional demeanor - Structured, independent and reliable working style - Organizational skills and high service orientation - Very good MS-Office skills and affinity for various tools and applications - Great interest in economic topics and the economic and political developments in the Canton of Grisons
