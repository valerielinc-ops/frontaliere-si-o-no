Role overview

Active participation in the training and supervision of assistants and senior doctors descrizione The Clinic for Orthopaedics, Traumatology and Hand Surgery today forms a wide-ranging musculoskeletal center at the Spital Limmattal.

It combines almost the entire spectrum of orthopaedics and traumatology of the musculoskeletal system with a special focus on modern endoprosthetics of the knee, hip and shoulder joint, including revision prosthetics, sports orthopaedics, shoulder surgery and foot and ankle surgery.

The range of services also includes the conservative and operative supply of injuries to the supporting and moving apparatus, in particular arthroscopic methods of large joints, and the treatment of complex fractures.
