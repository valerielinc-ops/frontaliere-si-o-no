Original description

Your environment: The Fürstenwald Therapy House offers up to 28 behaviorally challenging children and adolescents aged 7 to 16 years a care, education, and therapy offer. We operate two day and two residential groups with 6 to 7 places and 3 school classes for elementary, middle, and high school students. Your tasks: You teach approximately 29 lessons in the main and subsidiary subjects. You preferably have knowledge of foreign languages (English, Italian, and French). You participate in treatment and site meetings. You are in close contact with the public schools. You regularly exchange with the treatment team. Our requirements profile: You have the teaching diploma (high school/primary school teaching diploma). You have a degree in school-based rehabilitation or are ready to complete the training soon. You also take on the device responsibility in the entire school area (technical and operational use). You are engaged, flexible, resilient, and willing to take responsibility. You work well in a motivated teaching team and are characterized by good contact and communication skills. You are ready for interdisciplinary collaboration. We offer you: A compactly structured assignment in an easily manageable teaching team. Block teaching. Workload of 5 days per school week. Internal training and further education offer - we also support your external further education goals. We promote the compatibility of professional and private life.
