Role overview

Tasks: Are you familiar with the lively everyday life of an office, but at the same time would you like to discover something new?

Do you also enjoy supporting nursing staff in medication management?

With us you can expect a varied job in institutional care - combined with the charm of the local pharmacy.

Due to planned staff shortages in the coming months, we are looking for temporary support.

Your focus is on supporting our nursing institutions: you process orders, support nursing professionals by telephone and coordinate Medifilm orders.

You will be in regular contact with the individual institutions, answer questions competently and suggest suitable alternatives if necessary.

In addition, you will provide our customers in the local pharmacy with friendly and expert advice, process prescriptions responsibly and be actively involved in everyday pharmacy life.

You will also work in a structured and reliable manner in the back office and participate in the further development and optimization of our processes.

The position is temporary until the end of 2026, with an option for extension.
