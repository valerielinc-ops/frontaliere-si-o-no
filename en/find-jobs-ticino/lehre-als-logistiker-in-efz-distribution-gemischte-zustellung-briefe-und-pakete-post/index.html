Original description

Do you like to start early in the day, do you love being in the fresh air, and do you enjoy conjuring people a smile in the face? Then a training in the delivery (direction distribution) is just right for you! During the three-year basic training you will acquire the tool for a successful entry into the diverse world of logistics. Your training centre is located in Lausen or Reinach BL. Your training - In the early morning you will sort your letters and packages and load your delivery vehicle for the subsequent delivery. - After that, you will be on your own, bring and get shipments of any kind and take care of the concerns of our customers. - Before you start your well-deserved evening, you'll do some rework at your training center. - During your training, you will be supervised by professionals, introduced into practice and continually developed. - In addition, with financial assistance from the mail, you will either acquire the driver's license for A1 motorcycles or the driver's license for category B motor vehicles and learn how to run a truck. - With various internal stations, an operation in a warehouse, as well as individual selection modules according to your wishes, we complete your broad logistics training. That's what you bring We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, joy and motivation who want to move something. You should meet the following points: - Physical activity and work on the fresh air inspire you. - In addition, you have joy in dealing with people, are reliable, honest and silent. - You are ready to obtain the learning driving license A1 or B by the start of the apprenticeship. - compulsory schooling must be completed with sufficient grade average at the lowest level. - At the beginning of your apprenticeship, you have the language level B2 in German
