Main responsibilities

  • In your role, you are the first point of contact for clinical-pharmaceutical inquiries from the departments, whereby you can draw on the experience of the clinical pharmacists in the team at any time
  • To promote medication safety in the hospital, you support the departments with the creation of documents on the correct use of medication and take part in training for the doctors and nursing services
  • You contribute to a seamless supply of medication for patients by recommending adequate substitute products in the event of supply bottlenecks.
  • You will also coordinate batch recalls and forward important medication information to the relevant users.
  • You will conduct exit interviews for selected inpatients with the aim of improving adherence.
  • In addition, you will be involved in central issues of drug safety (antibiotic stewardship, TDM, interaction checks, pharmacovigilance, CIRS) with requirements:
  • You quickly familiarize yourself with existing processes and take on responsibility.
  • You are characterized by analytical thinking, an independent way of working and the ability to make decisions
  • You are a motivated, reliable and committed personality who enjoys working in a team and enjoys interdisciplinary collaboration
  • The concerns of customers and patients are your focus.
