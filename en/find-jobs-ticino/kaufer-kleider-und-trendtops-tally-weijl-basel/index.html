We're here to make you feel sexy, empowered & UNSTOPPABLE. Founded in 1984, TALLY WEiJL has been on a crazy journey to celebrate fashion, independence, fun, love, & community for close to 40 years. Today we’re present in 35 markets with about 400 stores, online presence, and 3m+ fans across social channels. Buyer – Dresses & Trend Tops About the Role We are looking for a Buyer with strong experience in Fast Track suppliers, able to translate the latest fashion trends into successful, fast-moving products. You will work closely with our Design Team to identify key trends and find the right suppliers to get the products as quickly as possible in stores. Your Responsibilities · Identify new trends in collaboration with the Design team. · Source and negotiate with Fast Track suppliers to ensure best time-to-market. · Manage the full buying process from concept to delivery. · Analyze product performance and react quickly to market needs. · Ensure the right product mix that reflects the brand’s DNA and drives sales. Your Profile · Proven experience as a Buyer, ideally within Fast Fashion / Trend-driven environment. · Deep understanding of the fast-track production model and supplier network. · Strong trend awareness and commercial sense. · Excellent negotiation and communication skills. · High adaptability, mental flexibility, and resilience in a dynamic environment. · Fluent in English (any additional language is a plus). Would you like to become a part of our fashion story and an enthusiastic member of our TALLY Family? Then share your individual talent with us and send us your application!
