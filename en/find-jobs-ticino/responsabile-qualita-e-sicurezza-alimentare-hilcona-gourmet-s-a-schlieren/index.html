Original description

Description Do you bring quality awareness, a sense of responsibility, and a passion for food? GOU (by Hilcona) is an innovative manufacturer of ultra-fresh food in Schlieren ZH, where we produce with great commitment and craftsmanship. We are looking for a complete and decisive personality as Quality Assurance and Quality Management Leader to start on 1st July 2026 or by agreement. In this key role, you are responsible for maintaining our high standards in product safety, quality, and legal requirements. You bring your expertise in the continuous development of our products. You work closely with various departments, implement hygiene regulations rigorously, conduct training, and internal and external audits. As an expert in our certifications and declarations/specifications, you will take on a central role in the quality of our products. ## Responsibilities - Responsible for product safety and quality throughout the entire process - Lead the local HACCP team and implement food safety regulations - Organize and conduct internal and external audits - Maintain existing certifications and ensure compliance - Train staff in quality and food safety - Create and maintain specifications, declarations, and article master data - Point of contact for authorities and external partners - Responsible for handling customer complaints - Responsible and monitoring of microbiological samples ## Requirements - Completed education in the food industry, preferably with further training in food technology, quality assurance, microbiology, or similar - High affinity for food - Experience in QS/QM of a food processing company - Knowledge of Swiss food legislation, standards, and labeling requirements - Experience in conducting and supervising internal and external audits - Experience in project management and an independent, structured way of working - Very good German language skills, English and French skills are a plus - High sense of responsibility, accuracy, and communication skills
