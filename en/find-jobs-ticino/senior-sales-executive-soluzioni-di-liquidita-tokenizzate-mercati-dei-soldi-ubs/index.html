Description

Are you interested in shaping the distribution of tokenized liquidity solutions at the intersection of traditional finance and digital asset innovation? Do you want to work with leading digital asset platforms to deliver institutional grade tokenized money market fund solutions? Are you motivated by developing new distribution channels in a rapidly evolving market environment? We’re looking for a Senior Sales Executive – Tokenized Liquidity Solutions (Money Market Funds) to:

  • drive global distribution of tokenized money market funds and liquidity solutions across the digital asset ecosystem
  • originate, structure and execute distribution partnerships with centralized and decentralized digital asset platforms, such as exchanges, wallets, custodians, stablecoin issuers, and DeFi participants
  • develop and execute the global go to market strategy for tokenized liquidity solutions
  • drive inflows into tokenized money market funds and related liquidity vehicles
  • advise partners on treasury, yield, settlement, and collateral use cases, including on and off chain liquidity solutions
  • act as a trusted advisor on liquidity management within digital asset markets, contributing insights to product development, pricing, and positioning
