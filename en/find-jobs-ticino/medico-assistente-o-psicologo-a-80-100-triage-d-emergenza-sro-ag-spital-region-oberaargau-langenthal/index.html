Role overview

The Psychiatric Services SRO ensure regional, community-based and patient-oriented psychiatric care for 80,000 residents.

We have modern, integrated care structures such as emergency triage, ward-equivalent mobile acute treatment, integrative psychiatric care, social psychiatric and psychotherapeutic outpatient clinics at 4 locations with special offers, as well as a rehabilitation day clinic, an acute day clinic, geriatric psychiatry with an outpatient clinic and a day clinic and an inpatient service with 34 beds in the Langenthal Hospital.

We have SIWF recognition as a category A training center for 3 years, both outpatient and inpatient.
