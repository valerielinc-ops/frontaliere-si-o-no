Original description

The company Rapelli has been synonymous with the Ticino Salumi tradition since 1929 and is known for high-quality products made from selected ingredients with a clear focus on quality, tradition and innovation. With the brands «Rapelli» and «Ticinella», the company is regarded as a culinary ambassador of Ticino in Switzerland. Rapelli is part of the ORIOR Group, an internationally active Swiss Food & Beverage company, which is listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange and focuses on high-quality specialities with strong regional anchoring.
