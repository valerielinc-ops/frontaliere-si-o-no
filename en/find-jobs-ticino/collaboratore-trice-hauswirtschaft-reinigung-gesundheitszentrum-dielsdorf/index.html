Dielsdorf Health Centre is a modern geriatric competence centre with around 500 employees from 35 nations.

Our offer ranges from consulting services for older people and their relatives, general and specialized long-term care to care and care at home.

With more than 200 beds, the stationary range of general long-term care at the Dielsdorf site is complemented by specialized treatment paths for rehabilitative acute care, palliative care, psychiatric geriatrics and the emphasis on dementia.
