Original description

Do you build strong partnerships and think strategically? Then become our Key Account Manager for the Retail sector in the Austrian market. In this exciting key role, you will be responsible for managing all Retail partners in Austria. Your focus will be on traditional trade – especially Spar and Billa. You will operate from Western Austria, working closely with our headquarters in Schaan. Tasks: You will analyze market, customer, and trend data in Austrian retail and derive effective measures You will strategically develop the business, identifying new potentials in customers and assortments, and continuously optimizing the existing portfolio You will plan and steer growth initiatives per market segment – in close coordination with Marketing, Competence Centers, Controlling, and Logistics You will take responsibility for budgets and targets, ensuring the achievement of volume, price, and distribution EBIT goals You will confidently manage internal and external interfaces, ensuring smooth implementation of projects and measures You will professionally and solution-oriented support our Key Accounts – including in the complaint process Requirements: You have a completed degree in business administration (university/university of applied sciences) or comparable training with relevant additional qualifications You bring several years of experience in the food sector within the Austrian retail landscape You have solid practical experience in Private Label Management – ideally complemented by knowledge in Marketing and Category Management You work independently, communicate convincingly, and prepare facts and results in a target group-oriented manner You think entrepreneurially and have a pronounced understanding of P&L You keep the strategically important levers in view without neglecting operational detail management You are a team player and work effectively and solution-oriented across departments
