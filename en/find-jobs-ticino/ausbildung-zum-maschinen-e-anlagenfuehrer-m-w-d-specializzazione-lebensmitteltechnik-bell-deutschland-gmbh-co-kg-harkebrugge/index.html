Original description

Are you technically enthusiastic about complex machines and plants and want to put your skills to the test in the production of high-quality food? Then we have the right ingredients! Tasks: During your training, you will learn on all production technical lines of the production process, how the result of your work can be appreciated. At various production lines, you will collect a lot of practical experience in setting up, monitoring and operating our production machines and, in the team, ensure a smooth production of our specialties, with care for established recipes and processes. In terms of product development, laboratory technology and product variety, we will make you fit for extracurricular training - so that the appetite for the job does not fade even after training. Requirements: Minimum good high school diploma Technical understanding and manual skill Concentrated and meticulous working style Reliability and enjoyment of teamwork
