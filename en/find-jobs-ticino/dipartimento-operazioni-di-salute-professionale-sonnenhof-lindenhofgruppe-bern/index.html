Original description

Are your sense of responsibility and self-initiative on your strengths? Would you like to be a member of a leading and successful team? Then fit us perfectly. As a Lindenhof group, we are only so successful because more than 800 nursing professionals, about 100 hospital doctors, 300 staff doctors and 1300 other employees work in hand – and can always rely on each other. The focus of high-quality medicine is always a strong team. And to strengthen this team we are looking for you! Tasks: They supply the operating rooms with the required material (screws, implants, consumables) and work closely with the specialist OP-Technik. • Supporting various professional groups within the framework of competences • Compensation of order and delivery documents • They support our logistics company in ordering and supplying our consumables for the Operations Department • They help to unpack the “Innight” packages and clean the implants and prostheses • Execution of cleansing and cleaning work Warehouse OPS • Regular working hours from Monday to Friday Requirements: • You have completed your training as a specialist • Experience in the field of surgical care is of advantage or great interest to work on • They work independently, quality-consciously and like to work in the team • Flexibility and good German skills complete your profile We offer: In the holistic care of our patients you have an outstanding role to play. They always keep an overview and make an indispensable contribution to the best possible therapy and care of patients with their functional skills. Thanks to ongoing investment in the infrastructure, you always work with state-of-the-art devices and can keep your professional know-how à jour. With your quality-conscious work, you can ensure the well-being of patients and experience the close, interdisciplinary cooperation with our doctors as well as our staff as an enrichment.
