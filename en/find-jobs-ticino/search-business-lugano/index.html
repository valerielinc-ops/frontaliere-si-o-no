More about this search for cross-border applicants

Lugano, the largest city in the Italian-speaking canton of Ticino, is a thriving hub for business professionals. With its strategic location near the Swiss-Italian border, Lugano attracts a diverse range of industries, including finance, international trade, and tourism. The city's vibrant economy offers numerous opportunities for business professionals, from entry-level positions to senior management roles. Lugano's unique blend of Swiss efficiency and Italian culture makes it an attractive destination for job seekers, particularly cross-border workers who commute from northern Italy. The city's excellent public transportation system and proximity to major European cities further enhance its appeal as a business center.
