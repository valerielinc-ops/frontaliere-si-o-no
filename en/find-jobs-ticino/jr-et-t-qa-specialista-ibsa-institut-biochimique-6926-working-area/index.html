Job Description We are seeking an OT Security Specialist to join our Corporate Cybersecurity team.

Main responsibilities

  • Monitor and protect industrial assets (PLC, SCADA, HMI) using the Claroty platform.
  • Perform Vulnerability Assessment and Risk Analysis specific to our pharmaceutical production environments.
  • Collaborate with the IT team to integrate OT telemetry into our SOC based on Microsoft Sentinel.
  • Ensure that our OT infrastructure meets international security standards and regulations (IEC 62443, NIST SP 800-82) and GxP/FDA guidelines.
  • Support the implementation of network segmentation strategies (Purdue model) and secure remote access management. Technical Requirements and OT Skills
  • Knowledge of industrial protocols (Modbus, Profinet, S7Comm, EtherNet/IP, BACnet, OPC-UA) and systems such as Siemens and Rockwell Automation.
  • Familiarity with the FactoryTalk suite.
  • Proven experience in using Claroty (xDome) and passive traffic analysis using third-party passive probes.
  • Basic knowledge of PLC programming logic methods.
  • In-depth knowledge of ICS/OT attack methods.
