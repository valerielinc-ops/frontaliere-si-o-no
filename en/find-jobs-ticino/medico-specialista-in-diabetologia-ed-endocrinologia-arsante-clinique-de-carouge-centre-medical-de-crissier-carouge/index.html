Description As part of the expansion of the Centre Médical Arsanté Crissier , we are recruiting a specialist in diabetes and endocrinology to complete our multidisciplinary team.

This extension project is part of our desire to strengthen the provision of care in the region and to develop coordinated care, especially for patients suffering from chronic diseases.

Located in the heart of Square Santé , a medical center in the Oassis district of Crissier that also includes Clinique La Source and Clinique de La Lignière, our center brings together health professionals in a modern and integrated environment, with radiology, laboratory, treatment rooms and rehabilitation services.
