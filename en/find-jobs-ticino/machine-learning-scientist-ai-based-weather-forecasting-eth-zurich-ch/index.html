Machine Learning Scientist (AI-based Weather Forecasting) 100%, Locarno, fixed-term print Drucken Recent advances in AI-based weather prediction have demonstrated remarkable skill and computational efficiency.

However, most current machine-learning weather prediction (MLWP) systems rely primarily on NWP analyses for initialization and only partially exploit the wealth of available satellite observations.

With the advent of the Meteosat Third Generation (MTG), new high-frequency and high-resolution measurements of clouds, moisture, temperature, and lightning activity provide unprecedented opportunities for substantially improving regional forecasts.
