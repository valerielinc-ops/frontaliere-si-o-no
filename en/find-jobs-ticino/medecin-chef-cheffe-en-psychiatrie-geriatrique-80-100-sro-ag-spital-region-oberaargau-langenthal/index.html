Role overview

The Geriatric Psychiatric Services SRO AG guarantee regional, community-based and patient-oriented psychiatric care.

We offer comprehensive geriatric psychiatric diagnostics and therapy for those aged 65 and over.

This includes outpatient consultation hours, a day clinic and consultative support for the Langenthal Hospital Center and regional retirement and nursing homes.
