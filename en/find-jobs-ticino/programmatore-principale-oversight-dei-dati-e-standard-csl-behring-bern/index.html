Principal Programmer

Clinical Data Oversight & Standards provides leadership and oversight for clinical data build, programming, and vendor deliverables across in-house, hybrid, and outsourced studies, ensuring alignment with CSL standards, regulatory requirements, and industry best practices.

The role drives governance, review, and validation processes; leads the implementation of CDASH and SDTM standards; and oversees scalable validation frameworks and data quality controls.
