Main responsibilities

  • Main tasks and responsibilities At EPFL, the automatic apprentice or the automatic apprentice will discover a complete trade combining mechanical, electronic and industrial computing.
  • He or she will participate in the manufacture of mechanical parts, both by manual machining and on machine tools.
  • The training will also include wiring and assembly of electrical plates, installation, commissioning and troubleshooting of automated machines, including robots and robotic systems.
  • The apprentice will be required to design mechanical components and prototypes using 3D modeling tools (CAO) and 3D printing.
  • He or she will develop and program Industrial Automation (PLC) and help integrate specialized automated systems, including smart sensors, electrical drives and robots.
  • The working environment is innovative and constantly evolving, at the heart of cutting-edge research.
  • As automation progresses rapidly, the apprentice will have the opportunity to become familiar with emerging technologies while developing concrete, interdisciplinary and highly sought after skills in an exciting and forward-looking sector.
