Original description

Location: Chur Function: Supply Chain Sub Function: Supply Chain - Production Type of Contract: Permanent For the beer production department at the Chur site (Southeast Switzerland), we are looking for an independent, self-assured, and team-oriented person. You report to the Manager Brewing and work in a structured, experienced team of around 12 brewers. Your area of responsibility includes: - Working in the brewhouse, fermentation and storage cellar, and filtration - Controlling the mostly automated facilities, identifying, analyzing, and solving any issues that arise - Ensuring the optimal production process - Participation in improvement measures according to the TPM (Lean) methodology - Support in the Technical Service for mechanical work and support in the Utilities area You bring the following qualifications: - Apprenticeship as a brewer or food technologist specialized in brewing - 2-3 years of professional experience, preferably in a brewery - Knowledge of an automated brewhouse (e.g., Brewmaxx) - Data management in modern information systems such as Proleit Integrate - Experience with modern control systems and good IT skills are required Your technical affinity and willingness to take on tasks beyond the typical profile of a brewer are desirable. Do you work well in a team? Are you proactive, resilient, punctual, and ready to work in a three-shift operation? Then we offer you interesting development opportunities. Interested? Then Claudia Tschuor Magnin looks forward to receiving your complete application documents. Respect, enjoyment, and quality from passion are our core values. As a beer producer with brewery locations in Chur and Lucerne as well as our own beverage trade with 17 locations throughout Switzerland, we are a company of national significance. We rely on a mix of tradition, innovation, and customer proximity. With our brand beers, we contribute to the diverse Swiss beer culture. We brew the joy of true togetherness to inspire a better world.
