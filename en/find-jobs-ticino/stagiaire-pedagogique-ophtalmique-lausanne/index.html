Role overview

Description Recognized as a competence center under the Law on Specialized Pedagogy, the CPHV promotes the inclusion of people with visual impairments in Romandy.

It is part of the Fondation Asile des aveugles, founded in Lausanne in 1843, which includes the Jules-Gonin Ophthalmic Hospital in Lausanne, the social and low vision rehabilitation service, the vision sciences research center, and the Clair-Soleil EMS in Ecublens, a medico-social facility specially adapted for elderly people with visual impairments.
