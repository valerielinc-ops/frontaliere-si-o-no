Company and context

  • The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required.
  • Thanks to the commitment and expertise of more than 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients.
  • The Department of Internal Medicine of the Regional Hospital of Bellinzona and Valli is looking for Doctors in Education (Internal Medicine) The Department of Internal Medicine of the Regional Hospital of Bellinzona and Valli (sedi di Bellinzona, Faido e Acquarossa) is an integral part of the Cantonal Hospital (www.eoc.ch).
  • It is recognized as a professional training center, category A, with a wide multidisciplinary case and involves employees, as well as in the field of care, even in those of research and training.
  • The peripheral hospitals located in Val Leventina (Faido) and Blenio valley (Acquarossa) complete the profile of the case in a dynamic of greater autonomy.
  • The medical team consists of 34 assistant doctors, 15 clinical chiefs and superiors belonging to different disciplines.
  • Profile: The person we seek has professional experience and autonomy, as well as motivation, own initiative and spirit of collaboration.
  • He believes, like us, in the importance of putting the patient and his family at the center of all our attentions.
  • Experience in a B or C training institute, in internal medicine preferably matured in Switzerland, or adequate clinical experience developed in other countries.
  • Good knowledge of the Italian language (B2) and if possible knowledge of a second national language (German and/or French) and English.
