Description At the Lengg Clinic we work in a large interdisciplinary team of almost 70 therapists with in-depth knowledge of various neurological disorders and treatment concepts.

The speech therapy team consists of 8 therapists, including our specialist for dysphagia and our student support team.

We attach great importance to regular interprofessional training and team meetings and offer plenty of space for professional exchange and individual support in a collegial environment.
