Original description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that's what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business now employs over 2500 people from more than 80 countries group-wide, with a growing trend. The company's headquarters are located in the canton of Glarus. Against a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. The «Retail» division includes over 250 chocolateries in more than 30 countries. Here, we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate in new ways again and again. Do you also want to be part of it? Then join us.
