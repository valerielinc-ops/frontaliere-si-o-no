We are looking for a **Junior Technical Visual Designer** to support the Marketing & Communication team in the development of high-quality visual materials and technical documentation. The role focuses on the creation of **3D models and realistic renderings of equipment and plant sections**, to be used across multiple communication channels such as videos, brochures, presentations, exhibitions and the corporate website.
