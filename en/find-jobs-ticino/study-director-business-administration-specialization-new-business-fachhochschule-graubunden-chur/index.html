Original description

As a university of applied sciences, the FH Graubünden emphasizes dynamic thinking and proactive action. We develop over 2,400 students in continuing and further education into responsible individuals. Our inter- and transdisciplinary research focuses on applied future technologies, development in alpine regions, and entrepreneurial action. As an employer, we promote equal opportunities and diversity. ## Tasks - You assume overall responsibility for the Master’s programme «Master of Science in Business Administration» with the specialization New Business and actively shape its strategic and content-related further development in a forward-looking manner. - You manage the programme operationally, ensure high quality, and take on personnel, organizational, and financial responsibility (including budget, semester, and timetable planning). - You oversee the admission and support of students and lecturers and develop innovative, practice-oriented teaching and examination formats. - You establish sustainable partnerships with business partners, alumni, and other stakeholders and are responsible for targeted marketing and the acquisition of new students. - You also engage in teaching at bachelor’s and master’s level and participate in applied research projects in the focus areas «Business and Supply Chain Transformation» and «AI for Business». - You ensure an effective transfer of current research results into teaching. ## Requirements - Consecutive master’s degree in Business Administration (doctorate advantageous) - Proven leadership experience, experience in budget responsibility, and preferably in-depth knowledge in the development and management of degree programmes - Research experience and practical expertise in Business and Supply Chain Transformation and ideally applications of AI in a business context - Experience in acquiring, leading, and implementing applied research projects, as well as a broad network in business and research - Didactic training and/or teach
