Role overview

Tasks

  • carry out technical-specialistic advice in horticulture and fruit growing
  • guarantee the weekly collection and transmission of data concerning the cantonal production of vegetables and berries in close collaboration with the Swiss horticulture plant and the Swiss Fruit Association
  • carrying out plant protection monitoring in collaboration with research centres as decision-making support for horticultural companies
  • organize training and information moments, manage working groups and of interest and draw up informative texts
  • implement fertilization plans, treatment plans, budgets, economic and agronomic relationships
  • accompany projects aimed at finding solutions for the garden sector adapted to our climate and territory
  • participate in meetings and collaborate at the cantonal and federal level with category associations and research to promote the cantonal orthogon sector
  • Requirements
  • university education (bachelor + master or bachelor) in agronomy or horticulture
  • experience in providing agricultural advice, with skills in horticulture and plant health monitoring
  • good knowledge of the cantonal territory
  • excellent knowledge of the Italian language (speaking and written) and excellent knowledge of at least one second official language (speaking and written), preferably German
  • interest in continuous learning, openness to professional challenges and attitude to interpersonal relationships
  • ability to assume responsibility and to work in a group
  • practical sense, ability to analyse and propose concrete solutions
  • initiative, discretion and reliability Special remarks
