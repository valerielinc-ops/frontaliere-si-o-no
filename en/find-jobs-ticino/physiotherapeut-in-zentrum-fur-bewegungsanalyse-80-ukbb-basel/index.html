Role overview

The Department of Neuroorthopaedics of the University Children's Hospital Beider Basel (UKBB) supervises patients and their families with a dynamic team in over 4500 consultations/years, including transition to the adult area of the University Hospital Basel (USB).

We are an expert center for orthopaedic deformities in rare neurological and neuromuscular diseases, and are thus part of several multidisciplinary speaking hours (with colleagues in spinal surgery, neurosurgery and various paediatric areas).

We have a state-of-the-art clinical trial laboratory for neuroorthopedic patients, as well as patients with general deformities, sports injuries and spinal pathologies.
