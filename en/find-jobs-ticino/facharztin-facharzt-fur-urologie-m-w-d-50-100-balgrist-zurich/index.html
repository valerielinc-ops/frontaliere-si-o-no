Role overview

The Balgrist University Hospital is one of the most renowned orthopedic clinics in Switzerland with international recognition.

To strengthen our neuro-urology team, we are looking for a committed specialist in urology with a focus on functional urology and neuro-urology.

The position is designed as a long-term permanent position and is particularly suitable for colleagues who are looking for stable, regulated clinical work, even part-time.
