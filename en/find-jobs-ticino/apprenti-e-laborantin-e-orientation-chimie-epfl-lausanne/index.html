Main responsibilities

  • Meeting with Sébastien, apprentice Laborantin in chemistry Main tasks and responsibilities The apprenticeship place is offered at the Central Environmental Laboratory (CEL) of the Faculty of Natural, Architectural and Built Environment (ENAC).
  • CEL is a multidisciplinary analytical platform active in the fields of environmental chemistry, molecular biology and microbiology.
  • With advanced analytical instruments, the laboratory develops and applies methods to characterize and quantify a wide range of chemical compounds and trace elements of major environmental interest.
  • CEL's research activities include studying the fate, transport and impact of pollutants in aquatic and terrestrial environments.
  • As an apprentice in chemistry, you will participate in a wide range of practical and analytical activities, including:
  • Prepare samples for analysis (extraction, digestion, filtration)
  • Learn to use high-tech analytical instruments (LC-MS/MS, GC-MS/MS, IC, ICP-OES, ICP-QQQ, etc.) for characterization and quantification of organic and inorganic substances.
  • Identify and characterize molecules using different methods of mass spectrometry.
  • Conduct analyses on environmental samples (water, soil, sediment), biological samples, as well as on various materials from EPFL laboratories or other research institutions.
  • Interpret analytical results to assess the accuracy, reproducibility and reliability of measurements.
