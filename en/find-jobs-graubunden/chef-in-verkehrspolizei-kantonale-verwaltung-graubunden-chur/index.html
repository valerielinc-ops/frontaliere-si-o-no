Descrizione The traffic police make a central contribution to road safety and the smooth flow of traffic. In this responsible management role, you lead a versatile organisational area with around 80 employees and actively shape the strategic development of the traffic police. By agreement, we are looking for a flexible and committed person as head of traffic police. ## Mansioni - Management of the Traffic Police as a police/on-call officer - Personnel, technical and organisational management of subordinate managers in the special and specialist areas (Traffic Base North, Traffic Registration, Traffic Technology, Traffic Administration, Heavy Traffic with the control centres Unterrealta (Cazis) and Campagnola (San Vittore) and the Traffic Competence Network - Ensuring traffic police supply and operational readiness in the geographically assigned area of responsibility - Supports the Head of Regional and Traffic Police in the Traffic Department - Supports the regional police as a specialist supervisor in the implementation of traffic police tasks - Close cooperation with the public prosecutor's office as well as other cantonal, intercantonal and national authorities and organisations - Further development of the organisation in the areas of structure, processes, competences and further training ## Requisiti - Completion ideal as a lawyer or comparable degree -
