Original description

Safety in Graubünden, for residents, tourists, conference participants and transients, peace and order for all. The cantonal policewomen and police officers take care of this. Day and night, year after year, in every weather condition, on mountain roads as well as on motorways, in town and countryside, in mountain and valley. They are always ready to assist, rescue, investigate, combat crime and prevent. The human being is always at the center. Within the framework of the strategy «Kapo GR 2025+», the promotion and development of employees, a future-oriented organization and the continuous optimization of core processes are at the center. As a new police commander, who will replace the current incumbent due to retirement on 1st April 2027 or by agreement, you will assume the overall responsibility for the cantonal police. It is your task to tackle future police-relevant challenges, in leadership, prevention and digitization.
