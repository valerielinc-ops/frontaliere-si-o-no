Role overview

Do you have an eye for detail, enjoy precise manual work, and have an interest in architecture and design?

Then you’ve come to the right place!

The Fachhochschule Graubünden is offering you an exciting and varied apprenticeship as an Architectural Model Maker EFZ starting on August 1, 2026.

  • Do you have an eye for detail, enjoy precise manual work, and have an interest in architecture and design?
  • Then you’ve come to the right place!
  • During your apprenticeship, you will create scale and true-to-life models of planned buildings and terrain.
  • You will work with various materials such as wood, plastic, metals, cork, and plaster, giving the models a realistic appearance with colors and plant-based materials.
  • Spatial imagination Keen sense of materials Enjoyment of practical and planning work Systematic thinking Precise work methods Motivated and eager-to-learn personality Creativity
  • Good academic performance in mathematics, drawing, and manual work
