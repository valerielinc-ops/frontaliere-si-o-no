Company and context

  • You like to get involved in the cultural, sporting or political areas This is what awaits you About us The Swiss Paraplegic Association (SPV), founded in 1980, is the national umbrella organization for paraplegics with around 11,000 members.
  • It promotes, represents and coordinates the concerns of paraplegics, quadriplegics and other members throughout Switzerland.
  • It also supports 27 regional wheelchair clubs throughout Switzerland.
  • The approximately 80 permanent employees and 170 freelance employees as well as more than 2,000 volunteers and volunteers are committed to the social, social, political and personal concerns of the members and are committed to their reintegration and improving the quality of life.
  • The range of services includes the institute for legal advice, wheelchair sports and leisure (promotion of elite and popular sports, as well as our own travel agency), the center for barrier-free construction and the life advice division.
  • The SPV also publishes the members' magazine Paracontact and two newsletters.
