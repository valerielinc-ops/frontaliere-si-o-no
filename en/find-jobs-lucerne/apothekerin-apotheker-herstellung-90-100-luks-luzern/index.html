Main responsibilities

  • In this position, you work in a GMP-regulated environment and in modern cleanrooms produce formula drugs for our patients as well as for partner hospitals.
  • The spectrum ranges from non-sterile to sterile drug forms; in the production of cytostatics a production robot from Loccioni is also used
  • In addition, you work closely with laboratory technicians as well as pharmacists from the pharmaceutical manufacturing sector.
  • Within the cytostatic production and the GMP production of small quantities, organize the daily business and take over plausibility checks as well as the release of regulations
  • In addition, your tasks include the interdisciplinary advice of doctors, doctors and nursing professionals in pharmaceutical matters, as well as the participation in ensuring the high quality standards of pharmacy.
  • Here you will create manufacturing regulations, work instructions and documents for qualifications and process validations and also participate in the piquetting service of the pharmacy Requirements:
  • For this position, you will bring a completed pharmacy study and ideally have expertise and experience in the production of pharmaceuticals in the GMP environment.
  • We also offer dedicated professional starters a thorough introduction
  • Personally convince you with an independent, reliable and flexible way of working and appreciate interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • Even in demanding situations, you remain resilient and enjoy taking responsibility
