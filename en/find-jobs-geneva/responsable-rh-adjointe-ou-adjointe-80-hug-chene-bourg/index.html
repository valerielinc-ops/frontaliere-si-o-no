Role overview

Mansioni - As an assistant human resources manager or assistant, you support the HR manager by ensuring the operational management of human resources in the Department of Psychiatry.

  • You are in charge of ensuring the proper application of HR processes in operational files, in accordance with HR policy, institution and applicable rules.
  • In a role of support and advice, you accompany local managers regarding the supervision of their teams and the evolution of skills needs.
  • You advise the collaborators of the target audience who are delegated to you during their professional career.
  • You intervene on the different stages of the staff life cycle (recruitment, mobility, departure).
  • You support the HR manager on complex and contentious situations.
  • Under the supervision of your manager, and alongside a team of 2 assistant and assistant HRR colleagues and 4 HR managers, you initiate innovative and adapted HR approaches with the main objective of developing the human capital of your department.
  • You represent the human resources function of the department in permanent committees, working groups, cross-cutting project teams and ensure the replacement of the human resources manager if necessary.
  • As part of the implementation of the HR strategy of the HUGs, you collaborate with the project teams in Axis 1: "care for employees within an institution that is closest to its values".
  • You support your manager in the development of a harmonious and respectful working environment for people.
