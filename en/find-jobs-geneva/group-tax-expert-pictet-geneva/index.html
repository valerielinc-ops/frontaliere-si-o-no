Role overview

Your team The Corporate Functions division brings together the Pictet Group's non-banking activities and has over 800 employees.

The core functions, which are made up of specialists with a perfect mastery of all business lines, are structured around several departments: Internal Audit, Communication, Compliance, Corporate Office, Finance, Taxation, Legal, Human Resources, Risk and Information Security.

Based in Geneva, the Group Tax department is responsible for managing the Group's tax situation, ensuring compliance with legal and regulatory requirements, managing tax risks, preserving Pictet's reputation and maintaining good relations with tax administrations.
