Application process

  • Working closely with global and regional teams, you will lead technology transfers , localize products to market and regulatory needs, and ensure full commercial readiness across sales, operations, and application support.
  • You will define the regional product strategy , together with local teams who will give the market insight, covering customer needs, competition, pricing, and regulations.

  • Acting as a product application expert, you will work closely with customers and internal specialists, support pilots and early deployments, and turn feedback into continuous product improvements.
