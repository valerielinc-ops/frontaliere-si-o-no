Role overview

chevron left Overview ICT-System specialist:in (m/w/d) 100%, Zurich, unlimited print Print To support research and teaching, ETH Zurich's computer science services act as a service organization in a large and complex IT environment.

We offer high-quality, flexible and scalable computer science services to members of ETH Zurich and support them in competition with the best universities in the world.

The ID Customer Experience and Solutions (CxS) section is responsible for user support and IT workstation management in research departments, central administrative units and ETH-close units.

In addition, CxS manages the central computer workspaces and operates the multifunction printers at ETH.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for an experienced and committed personality as an ICT System specialist:in (m/w/d).

In this versatile position, you are responsible for the planning, development and smooth operation of our heterogeneous IT infrastructure.

You sleep the bridge between the most modern technology and excellent research.

  • System engineering: You manage and optimize our client and server infrastructure (Linux & Windows) as well as the GPU systems for research.
  • Support & Infrastructure: Ensure the operation of IT workstations and specialist laboratory IT and provide competent support.
  • Innovation through automation: With scripting and modern tools you automate processes and develop our IT services proactively.
