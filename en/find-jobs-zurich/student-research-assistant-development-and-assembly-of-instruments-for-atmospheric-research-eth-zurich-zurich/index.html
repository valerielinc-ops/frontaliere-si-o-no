Role overview

Student Research Assistant – Development and assembly of instruments for atmospheric research 10%-30%, Zurich, fixed-term print Drucken The Atmospheric Physics group at ETH Zurich is looking for a Student Research Assistant to support the development and assembly of instruments used during field campaigns .

Our research group studies the crucial role of clouds and aerosols in the climate system, aiming to better understand how cloud microphysical processes drive both precipitation formation and the radiative impact of clouds.

Project background This role directly supports our research efforts by building the instruments we deploy in the field.
