Application process

  • The "Studio Foundations" focuses on students and orchestrates the application of disciplinary knowledge and skills in the design over two semesters.
  • Through direct and sensual access, students acquire basic concepts such as context and landscape, climate and energy, light and space, meaning and form as well as material and structure and understand their influence on architectural design.
  • The course emphasizes interdisciplinary and thus critical thinking by combining abstract concepts with real experiences in different standards – from living spaces to urban landscapes.
  • Jacqueline Pauli's chair is responsible for providing basic knowledge to the entire range of building materials.
  • This takes place over 2-3 weeks of focus in the autumn semester as well as a direct transfer to the design projects.
