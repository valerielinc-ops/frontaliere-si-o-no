Tasks

Process and data quality

  • Ensuring and further developing administrative processes along the patient journey, in particular in the areas of performance detection, (pre-)coding, flawing and medical controlling
  • Ensuring correct and complete performance detection (Tarpsy, Tardoc) through technical clarifications and binding consultations with the medical-therapeutic areas
  • Monitoring and continuous improvement of data quality
  • Implementation of necessary adaptations due to external requirements

  • Designing and developing roles, responsibilities and processes in the field of performance and data management
  • Training of employees involved in performance and data management
  • Establishment and maintenance of instruments for quality assurance and process control (e.g. key figures, data quality analyses and evaluations)
  • Design and parameterization of tariff-relevant processes in the system as well as ensuring a functioning interface management for continuous improvement of data quality The function combines process understanding, collection of structured data and tariff-relevant requirements with the further development of cross-organisational processes along the patient journey. They work closely with medical-therapeutic areas, finance, IT and administration. As a specialist, you have the technical responsibility for a uniform and reliable performance and data management. They set standards, clarify interfaces and translate tariff, administrative and medical-therapeutic requirements into sustainable processes for everyday clinics.

  • Multiannual experience in the administrative field of Swiss healthcare, ideally in stationary or outpatient psychiatry
  • Funded knowledge in the fields of performance detection, coding, facsification or controlling in Swiss healthcare
  • High communication and interface competence and joy in interprofessional cooperation with different professional groups
  • Extensive interest in data quality, process optimization and inter-organisational relationships
  • Conceptual strength combined with pragmatic implementation competence
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented mode of operation
  • IT-Affinity
