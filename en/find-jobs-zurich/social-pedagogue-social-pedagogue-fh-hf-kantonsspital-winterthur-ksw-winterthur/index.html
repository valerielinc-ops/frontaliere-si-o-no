Role overview

The social paediatric centre (SPZ) is an organizational unit of the Centre for Children and Youth Medicine of the Canton Hospital Winterthur.

The stationary psychosomatic (KJPP) at the SPZ is a special care area for children and youth psychiatry.

The stationary Department of Children's / Youth Psychiatry and Psychosomatics treats 12-14 children and young people aged 8-18 who are hospitalized due to psychosomatic and mental illnesses (e.g.

eating disorders, somatization and anxiety disorders as well as school absentism).

The care of children and young people is carried out during the day by an interdisciplinary team consisting of nurses and/or social pedagogues/social pedagogues in close cooperation with other professional groups in the milieutheraupeutical setting.
