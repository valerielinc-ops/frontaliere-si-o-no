Role overview

For our IT department we are looking for a/n System Engineer -/in (2nd level support) As a system engineer, together with our ICT team, you are responsible for the maintenance, development and renewal of our diverse systems such as virtualization (VMware) networks, Apple ecosystem (JAMF), security awarness, Azure and Office 365 and other applications.

In this function, you are also responsible for the analysis and elimination of IT issues in the 2nd level support and contribute to a stable operation of our IT infrastructure.

In project teams, you plan, develop and integrate new services and technologies.

You also support the 1st level support for demanding technical requests.

We need a good technical understanding, an independent working method and the motivation to work on new topics.

We offer you a modern working environment, varied tasks and the opportunity to actively contribute and develop your experiences.

With its individual quality of life, each individual person is in the centre.

We are committed to the fact that our around 250 residents, 246 day-care facilities and 52 employees at sheltered jobs in the Wagerenhof can enjoy the highest possible quality of life.

The Wagerenhof is an attractive employer and a competent training partner for learners of all levels.

For this we give you the responsibility

  • Together with our ICT Operations Team, you will support our IT systems
  • You are co-responsible for the continuous guarantee of security.
