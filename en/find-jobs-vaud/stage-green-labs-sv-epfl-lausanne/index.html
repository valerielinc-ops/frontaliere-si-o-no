Role overview

Mission Your mission is to support the activities of the SV Sustainability Office, which is working on the implementation of the Green Labs Vision EPFL Description The Sustainability Office of the Faculty of Life Sciences (SV) is responsible for environmental sustainability within the faculty at EPFL.

We work within the decanate and are in touch with the scientific, administrative and student community of the SV faculty to integrate the "Green Lab" vision.

This vision is to conduct environmentally and socially responsible research without compromising the quality of the research.
