Role overview

Mission As Deputy EM-PP / Head of Quality Assurance, you are piloting the EPFL quality system and ensuring the preparation and follow-up of institutional accreditation.

As such, you advise the Directorate in defining, implementing and monitoring its quality assurance policy.

You support the Presidency teams in the quality control and drafting of various strategic and thematic documents.
