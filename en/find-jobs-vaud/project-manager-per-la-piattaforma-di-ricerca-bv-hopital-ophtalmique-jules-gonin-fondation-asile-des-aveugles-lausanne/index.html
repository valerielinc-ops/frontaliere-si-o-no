Role overview

Founded in Lausanne in 1843, the Asile des Aveugles Foundation includes the Jules-Gonin Ophthalmic Hospital, a university ophthalmology service, a research center, the Pedagogical Center for Visually Impaired Students (CPHV), social services, rehabilitation and low vision services, as well as the Clair-Soleil medical-social institution.

Over 650 employees work daily to serve visual health and provide diagnostic, treatment and support services to people affected by visual impairment.
