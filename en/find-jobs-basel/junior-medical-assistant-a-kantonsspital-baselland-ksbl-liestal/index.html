Main responsibilities

  • You will learn to deal with patients and their relatives on the acute department for General Internal Medicine.
  • Collecting anamnesis and status from internal medicine patients.
  • Acquiring manual skills under supervision such as pleura, ascites, or lumbar punctures, blood gas analysis, wound care, and placement of venous lines.
  • Rotation possibilities to the emergency station and to the medical outpatient clinic.
  • Learning administrative medical tasks such as writing the patient history, progress entries and reports, case presentations at meetings, visits and further training.
