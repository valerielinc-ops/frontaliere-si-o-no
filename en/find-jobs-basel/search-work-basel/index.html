More about this search for cross-border applicants

Basel, a vibrant city in northwestern Switzerland, offers a dynamic job market with diverse opportunities for cross-border workers. Known for its strong pharmaceutical, chemical, and financial sectors, Basel attracts professionals from neighboring Italy, particularly those commuting from the Lombardy and Piedmont regions. With a high quality of life and excellent public transportation, Basel is an ideal location for frontalieri seeking employment in Italian-speaking Switzerland. The city's trinational nature, shared with France and Germany, further enriches its cultural and economic landscape.
