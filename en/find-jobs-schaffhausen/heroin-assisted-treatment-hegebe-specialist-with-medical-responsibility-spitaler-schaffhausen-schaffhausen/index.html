Role overview

For our heroin-assisted treatment (HeGeBe) in the psychiatric services, we are looking for a qualified nurse HF, health specialist or medical practice assistant immediately or by appointment.

The HeGeBe (heroin-assisted treatment) Schaffhausen is one of 23 treatment centers in Switzerland.

HeGeBe offers outpatient opioid agonist therapies and supports people with addiction disorders in improving their somatic, psychiatric and social situation, preventing infections such as HIV or hepatitis, reducing criminal offenses and long-term abstinence from non-prescribed substances.
