Main responsibilities

  • Develop and implement clinical guidelines and care programs specific to the TCA program, ensuring the quality and consistency of the practices
  • Direct complex diagnostic and therapeutic decisions, integrating the somatic, psychiatric and psychological dimensions of TCAs (anorexia, bulimia), from childhood to adulthood (6-66 years)
  • Participate in clinical reflections on therapeutic indications and treatment orientations within the multidisciplinary team
  • Collaborate closely with all professionals of the TCA program (psychologists, nurses, dietaryists, somatic doctor, pediatricians)
  • Ensure active coordination between the various internal and external interveners
  • Provide an experience and support specialized in treatment teams
  • Contribute to the development and improvement of TCA-specific diagnostic and therapeutic methods
  • Participate in team meetings, clinical supervision and service institutional activities (training, working groups)
  • Bring ethical and institutional reflection on situations with high complexity Skills required
  • Knowing the somatic and psychiatric complications of TCAs as well as the specialized refuel and follow-up protocols

Key requirements

  • FMH specialist title in psychiatry or pediatrics required.

  • Ability to train and supervise an interdisciplinary team.
  • Expertise in somatic and psychiatric complications of TCA.
  • Experience in managing eating disorders.
  • Collaborate within healthcare system networks.
  • Minimum of 3 years of experience in hospital or outpatient psychiatry.
