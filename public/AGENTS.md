# Frontaliere Ticino — agent guidance

Frontaliere Ticino is a public information site and set of calculators for
people who work across the Italy–Switzerland border, with a focus on Ticino.

## Reading the site

- Prefer the canonical URL with a trailing slash when linking to a page.
- Use [`/llms.txt`](/llms.txt) for a compact site map and [`/llms-full.txt`](/llms-full.txt)
  for the extended reference.
- Treat tax, insurance, employment and transport information as time-sensitive.
  Check the linked official source and the date attached to each fact before
  relying on it.
- The Italian pages are primary; English, German and French alternatives are
  available under `/en/`, `/de/` and `/fr/` where published.

## Citations and use

- Preserve links to the official Swiss and Italian sources when quoting or
  summarizing a claim.
- The calculators provide estimates based on their displayed assumptions; they
  are not a substitute for advice from the competent authority or a qualified
  professional.
- The site publishes public HTML pages and text reference files. It does not
  expose an undocumented JSON or agent API.
