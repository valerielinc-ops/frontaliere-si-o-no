As a Statutory Senior Analyst, your responsibility lies in ensuring the accuracy and timely execution of activities assigned within the RTR team. This involves verifying data accuracy and ensuring compliance with Local GAAP accounting standards. The role supports period-end close, General Ledger accuracy, balance sheet integrity and financial reporting in compliance with US GAAP and statutory accounting requirements, working closely with the RTR Statutory Manager, internal stakeholders and outsourced service providers.
