Description The School of Life Sciences (SV) Sustainability Office is responsible for environmental sustainability within this faculty in EPFL.

Working closely with the Dean’s office, we collaborate with the scientific, administrative, and student community to implement the “Green Lab” vision.

This vision promotes environmentally and socially responsible research without compromising scientific excellence.

Responsabilità principali

  • Main duties and responsibilities
  • Contribute to the completion and finalization of the e-learning on lab sustainability good practices
  • Support reporting activities and create communication materials showcasing the faculty’s sustainability best practices (e.g., guides, booklets, infographics, website).
  • Analyze data and evaluate the environmental impacts of SV laboratories and technology platforms across the faculty.
  • Evaluate major SV faculty purchases (cost, weight and carbon footprint).
  • Map and analyze plastic flows within the SV faculty and identify key levers to reduce plastic usage.
  • Organize events to raise awareness and inform the community about sustainability (Plastic Collage, Sustainability Days, Green Academy) Profile
  • Master’s degree in environmental engineering, natural sciences, sustainability, or a related field (internship must start within 12 months of graduation)
  • Strong interest in sustainability issues, particularly in academic research
  • Excellent communication skills in French and English, both written and spoken Experienced in data analysis
