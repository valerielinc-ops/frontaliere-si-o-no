# Instagram performance review

Use the sanitized native performance brief and scorecard supplied by the
weekly workflow. They contain only eligible, thresholded aggregates. Keep
channel, format, measurement window, assignment mode, and experiment cohort
separate. Missing metrics are unknown, not zero. Describe observed patterns;
do not claim causation or a winning hook unless the frozen experiment protocol
explicitly qualifies it.

Do not scrape Instagram, browse competitor accounts, collect public reel
metrics, download videos, or send data to a third-party swipe-file tool. The
workflow has no approved competitor-data collection path. If the private
brief is unavailable, offer source-inventory ideas or clearly label a
hypothesis; do not call it a trend or viral format.

Translate an eligible descriptive pattern into a small editorial hypothesis
for a future pack refresh. Do not increase cadence, change experiment
assignment, or promote a winner from descriptive evidence alone.
