# Fonti: Stato del permesso e dati personali nel CV

Pagine lette il 3 ottobre 2026. Ricerca e verifica sono di due agenti distinti: il secondo ha riaperto ogni
fonte e cercato di confutare ogni affermazione. Le citazioni sono nella lingua della fonte; il testo della
ricerca è in inglese, come è stato prodotto. Le **decisioni** prese su queste fonti sono in
[`../decisioni.md`](../decisioni.md): dove la raccomandazione del ricercatore e la decisione differiscono,
vale la decisione.

Domanda: How a cross-border candidate who holds no Swiss permit yet should state the work-authorisation status in a CV, truthfully: rules of the permit G (who applies, when, documents, job requirement, validity), the three-month / 90-day notification, entitlement of EU/EFTA nationals versus third-country nationals, weekly return and residence condition, existing CV guidance, and a closed list of status options with wording in German, French, Italian and English

## Esito della verifica

- Affermazioni: 89; verdetti: 89 confirmed.
- La raccomandazione regge: sì.

### Obiezioni del verificatore

- All 89 quotes were found on the cited pages (46 sources opened; three needed a cookie, two URLs redirect). The core holds: a closed list, the plain line 'nationality (EU), residence in country' for an EU/EFTA national without a permit, no wording that promises a permit, and nationality only for third-country nationals. The objections below concern two options that fail the 'true for everybody who picks it' test, several summary sentences that go beyond the sources, and wording mechanics.
- Option permit_g fails the test as specified. Its condition is 'valid today', but the researcher's own sources disagree on when a G permit stops being valid after a job ends: St. Gallen says at the end of the employment (C14), Ticino says six months later (C11), Geneva says to destroy it only on definitive cessation of all activity. No federal rule was found, and Art. 61 AIG does not list the end of employment. A former commuter with an unexpired card, the typical person writing a CV, cannot tell whether the line is true. Fix: offer permit_g only on a fact the candidate can check ('I am currently employed in Switzerland under this permit'); everybody else living abroad gets the plain nationality-and-residence line.
- Option eu_efta_abroad_explicit should be dropped. (a) It inherits the same uncertainty: a Ticino ex-commuter within six months may still hold a valid permit, so 'ohne Schweizer Bewilligung' can be false. (b) The four versions do not say the same thing: the German says no permit of any kind; the French, Italian and English say no work permit. (c) No source asks for a negative statement; the guides say to state a permit if there is one (C66, C80). (d) It suggests a missing requirement, although for EU/EFTA nationals no permit can exist before a job and none is needed up to three months (C05, C21, C39).
- The basis given for eu_efta_abroad is wrong and its condition is too wide. Point 3 says nationality and residence abroad 'define a cross-border commuter under the Agreement'. Annex I Art. 7 para. 1 (C58) also requires employment in Switzerland and the weekly return, and the SEM requires residence in an EU/EFTA state (C01). Condition (2) accepts any country outside Switzerland, so an Italian living in London could pick it. The printed line stays true, but the review page must not label the option 'cross-border commuter', and the condition should either require residence in an EU/EFTA state or drop the commuter rationale.
- The permit options are true 'on the day the CV is generated', but a CV is reused for months. An L permit follows a contract of at most 364 days and a G permit follows the job. The product needs the expiry date or a re-confirmation at each export; otherwise a stored line can become false.
- Third-country holders of a G permit: printing 'Grenzgängerbewilligung G' in a CV sent to a new employer is literally true but can be read as transferable. Aargau says a job change needs a new permit and is as a rule not possible, and the SEM limits the permit to the issuing canton's border zone (C45). The recommendation only says 'type only'. Either do not offer permit_g to third-country nationals or show a warning on the review page.
- Several summary sentences go beyond the sources. (a) 'A permit G EU/EFTA exists only with a job': self-employed commuters also receive one (C01, SEM factsheet). (a) 'by the employer in St. Gallen and Basel-Stadt, with the worker's own steps in Ticino and Graubünden': the Basel-Stadt page addresses the worker as applicant and the Graubünden page does not say who files; the federal directive treats the EU/EFTA national as applicant (II 2.1.3, II 2.2.2), which the gap 'no single federal sentence settles it' overlooks. (e) 'Every institutional CV guide says nationality plus the permit actually held': Vaud says 'nationalité ou permis de séjour' (C74), EURES lists no permit (C75), SECO lists neither (C76). Point 2, 'lapses when the job ends or six months later, depending on the canton', generalises from two leaflets.
- The 'omit' option is permitted but weakly supported for foreign applicants. BIZ Bern calls such information voluntary only if it is not relevant to the job and its absence raises no questions (the sentence before C68); the jobs.ch guide tells applicants with only a foreign nationality to add both items (C77); Randstad lists the omission as a common mistake (C84). Also, 'recommended default' must mean a suggestion on the review page: no option may be pre-selected and printed without the candidate's choice.
- The wordings are given for one example only (an Italian national living in Italy). The code needs per-country forms in four languages or it prints wrong text: German 'Wohnsitz in den Niederlanden', 'in der Slowakei'; French 'domicile au Portugal', 'aux Pays-Bas', 'à Malte'; Italian 'residenza nei Paesi Bassi', 'a Malta'; English 'resident in the Netherlands'; plus the nationality adjectives in French, Italian and English. The EFTA tag is '(AELE)' in French and '(AELS)' in Italian (C87, C88); the options show only '(EU)' and '(UE)'. The German form with the country name ('Nationalität: Italien') was not checked against any Swiss template, as the researcher admits.
- Point 1 says nothing is inferred from the address, yet eu_efta_abroad prints a country of residence. The candidate must confirm that country explicitly, and the option must be blocked or flagged when the CV address is in Switzerland; otherwise the CV contradicts itself.
- The researcher missed that commercial cross-border guides already tell EU citizens to print an entitlement line: 'Anspruch auf G/B-Bewilligung (EU-Bürger)' and 'Éligible au permis G/B (Ressortissant UE)' (ibani, updated 30.09.2026). The gap 'only one source addresses the no-permit case' is therefore wrong. The ban in point 4 still stands on the SEM directive (C31, C32, C33), but candidates will ask for this wording, so the review page should say why it is not offered.
- Smaller points. The basis cited for 'swiss' (C72, C77) does not support printing 'Nationalität: Schweiz': ETH asks Swiss nationals for the place of origin and jobs.ch asks them for nothing; berufsberatung.ch (C64) does support it. Point 8 (cover letter) has no source; it is a policy choice. Holders of F, N, S or Ci permits and EU/EFTA nationals living in Switzerland without a permit only get 'nationality only' or 'omit'; acceptable for a cross-border product, but the review page should say so. The fixed EU/EFTA list needs an 'as of' date, because the Agreement is extended to a new EU member only by protocol.

## Affermazioni e fonti

### C01 (T1) — verifica: confirmed

The G EU/EFTA permit concerns EU/EFTA nationals who live in an EU/EFTA state and work in Switzerland (job or self-employment).

- Fonte: SEM (Staatssekretariat für Migration) — <https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/de/home/themen/aufenthalt/eu_efta/ausweis_g_eu_efta.html>
- Citazione: «Als Grenzgängerin und Grenzgänger werden Staatsangehörige der EU/EFTA bezeichnet, die sich in einem EU/EFTA-Staat aufhalten und in der Schweiz arbeiten (Stellenantritt oder Aufnahme einer selbstständigen Erwerbstätigkeit).»
- Nota del verificatore: Quote is on the SEM page (last change 01.01.2022).

### C02 (T1) — verifica: confirmed

Validity: five years, provided there is an employment contract that is open-ended or longer than one year.

- Fonte: SEM (Staatssekretariat für Migration) — <https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/de/home/themen/aufenthalt/eu_efta/ausweis_g_eu_efta.html>
- Citazione: «Die Bewilligung für Grenzgängerinnen und Grenzgänger aus der EU/EFTA ist fünf Jahre gültig, sofern ein Arbeitsvertrag vorliegt, der unbeschränkt oder länger als ein Jahr gültig ist.»

### C03 (T1) — verifica: confirmed

Italian wording of the validity rule: five years with an open-ended or >1 year contract, otherwise the duration of the contract.

- Fonte: SEM (Segreteria di Stato della migrazione) — <https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/it/home/themen/aufenthalt/eu_efta/ausweis_g_eu_efta.html>
- Citazione: «Il permesso per frontalieri UE/AELS è valevole cinque anni purché sussista un contratto di lavoro di durata indeterminata o superiore a un anno. Se il contratto è stipulato per meno di un anno, la durata di validità del permesso corrisponde a quella del contratto.»

### C04 (T1) — verifica: confirmed

The employer is entered in the cross-border permit: the permit is tied to an actual employment relationship.

- Fonte: SEM, Factsheet «Grenzgängerinnen und Grenzgänger» (SP_01/22) — <https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/eu/fza/personenfreizuegigkeit/factsheets/fs-grenzgaenger.pdf.download.pdf/fs-grenzgaenger.pdf>
- Citazione: «Bei Bestehen eines einjährigen oder länger dauernden Arbeitsverhältnisses wird eine Grenzgängerbewilligung mit einer Gültigkeitsdauer von fünf Jahren ausgestellt. Der Arbeitgeber wird im Grenzgängerausweis eingetragen.»
- Nota del verificatore: Quote found in the factsheet SP_01/22 (hyphenated at line breaks). The same factsheet also gives a five-year permit to self-employed commuters, so 'tied to an employment relationship' holds for employees only.

### C05 (T1) — verifica: confirmed

The cross-border permit is linked to an employer and cannot be obtained in advance to help a job search; a candidate who has no Swiss job normally holds no permit G.

- Fonte: République et canton de Genève (OCPM), page mise à jour le 4 juin 2025 — <https://www.ge.ch/demander-permis-travail-frontalier/activite-salariee-frontalier-ue/aele>
- Citazione: «Par ailleurs, l'autorisation de travail frontalière étant liée à un employeur, il n'est pas possible d'obtenir un permis en vue de faciliter une recherche d'emploi.»
- Nota del verificatore: Geneva page, updated 4 June 2025. The second half of the claim is the researcher's inference.

### C06 (T1) — verifica: confirmed

When (Geneva): the application must be filed before the activity starts.

- Fonte: République et canton de Genève (OCPM) — <https://www.ge.ch/demander-permis-travail-frontalier/activite-salariee-frontalier-ue/aele>
- Citazione: «Votre demande d'autorisation de travail doit être déposée avant de commencer votre activité.»

### C07 (T1) — verifica: confirmed

- Fonte: République et canton de Genève (OCPM) — <https://www.ge.ch/demander-permis-travail-frontalier/activite-salariee-frontalier-ue/aele>

- Fonte: République et canton de Genève (OCPM) — <https://www.ge.ch/demander-permis-travail-frontalier/activite-salariee-frontalier-ue/aele>

### C09 (T1) — verifica: confirmed

### C10 (T1) — verifica: confirmed

Ticino, EU/EFTA holders: right to change job with a valid permit without waiting for an authorisation (the change must be notified before starting); the same notice says the permit is valid in all of Switzerland.

- Fonte: Repubblica e Cantone Ticino, Ufficio della migrazione (Avvertenze permesso G UE/AELS, 09/2024) — <https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DI/DI_DI/SEZPOP/documentazione/avvertenze/2017/G_Avvertenze_Cittadini_UE-27_AELS_e_datori_di_lavoro.pdf>
- Citazione: «Diritto a cambiare posto di lavoro con permesso valido (entro 6 mesi dalla cessazione della precedente attività) senza attendere alcuna autorizzazione da parte dell'Autorità.»
- Nota del verificatore: Item 4 of the notice (September 2024); item 1 says 'valido in tutta la Svizzera'.

### C11 (T1) — verifica: confirmed

Ticino: the EU/EFTA permit G loses validity on expiry, six months after the gainful activity ceases, or on revocation.

- Fonte: Repubblica e Cantone Ticino, Ufficio della migrazione (Avvertenze permesso G UE/AELS, 09/2024) — <https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DI/DI_DI/SEZPOP/documentazione/avvertenze/2017/G_Avvertenze_Cittadini_UE-27_AELS_e_datori_di_lavoro.pdf>

### C14 (T1) — verifica: confirmed

- Fonte: Kanton St.Gallen, Migrationsamt (Merkblatt 09/2026) — <https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/sicherheit/migration/dokumente_einreise,-aufenhalt-und-ausreise/eu-efta/einreise-eu-efta/grenzg%C3%A4nger/Merkblatt%20erwerbstaetige%20Grenzgaengerinnen_Grenzgaenger%20(EU%20EFTA).pdf>

## Raccomandazione del ricercatore

WHAT THE SOURCES ESTABLISH
(a) A permit G EU/EFTA exists only with a job. It is tied to an employer and cannot be obtained for a job search [C04, C05, C36]. It is requested before work starts [C06, C09, C12, C32]: by the employer in St. Gallen and Basel-Stadt, with the worker's own steps in Ticino and Graubünden, online in Geneva and Vaud, where the permit is then sent to the employer [C08, C09, C12, C15, C16, C17, C18]. The documents are an identity document and the employer's declaration or contract, plus cantonal extras [C07, C13, C17, C37]. It lasts five years, or as long as the contract [C02, C03].
(b) Up to three months or 90 days per calendar year there is no permit at all: the employer notifies online, at the latest the day before [C21-C27].
(c) EU/EFTA nationals have a legal entitlement if the conditions are met, but the SEM says in the same paragraph that no guarantee can be given and that the grant is subject to public order [C29-C35]. Third-country nationals only have a "can" rule: the employer's application, a durable right of residence in a neighbouring state, six months in the border zone and the priority test; the permit lasts one year, is limited to the canton's border zone and each job change needs authorisation. No quota applies to the permit G; quotas concern L and B permits [C44-C56].
(d) The weekly return applies to everybody. EU/EFTA nationals may live anywhere in the EU/EFTA; third-country nationals only in the border zone [C58-C63].
(e) Every institutional CV guide says "nationality plus the permit actually held"; none covers a candidate without a permit [C64-C76]. The portals say one can apply without a permit and that the status must be stated honestly [C78, C79, C82].

WHAT THE PRODUCT SHOULD DO (my recommendation, not a documented convention)
1. Offer the closed list below. The candidate picks one option on the review page and the code prints the line. Nothing is inferred from the address or the nationality, except the tag in point 3.
2. Show the permit options (C, B, L, G) only behind the label "I hold this permit and it is valid today". A permit G follows the job [C04] and lapses when the job ends or six months later, depending on the canton [C11, C14]. Print the permit name in full, never a bare letter: B and C are also driving-licence categories [C69, C85]. Use the SEM names [C86-C89].
3. For an EU/EFTA national who lives abroad and has no Swiss permit, print facts only: the nationality with the tag and the country of residence, "Nationalität: Italien (EU), Wohnsitz in Italien". The code derives the tag from the fixed list EU-27 plus Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway [C42, C43]; the United Kingdom is not in it [C28, C41]. These two facts define a cross-border commuter under the Agreement [C58, C59]. The line is true for everybody who picks it and says nothing about a permit. If the owner wants the absence of a permit spelled out, use the variant ending in "ohne Schweizer Bewilligung" (same condition). Offer one of the two, not both.
4. Never print, for anybody, that a permit will be granted, is guaranteed or is a formality. Excluded wordings: "Anspruch auf Bewilligung", "diritto alla libera circolazione", "Bewilligung beantragt / in Bearbeitung", "permesso G da richiedere", "permis G à demander", "keine Bewilligung nötig". The entitlement depends on a contract and on public order [C31, C32, C33, C36]. Nothing can be pending before there is a job [C05]. For a job of up to three months no permit is involved [C21, C24], so even "G to be requested" is not true for everybody. For the same reasons do not adopt the recruiter's advice in C83.
5. Third-country nationals. This group includes UK nationals, holders of an Italian, French, German or Austrian residence title, and family members of EU citizens [C41, C57].
   - Do NOT offer "eu_efta_abroad".
   - Do NOT print "(EU)" or "(EFTA)".
   - Do NOT print anything about a future permit, eligibility or "no quota", and do not present the foreign residence title as if it were a Swiss authorisation.
   - Print the nationality only, or the Swiss permit actually held, and then the type only. Never add "gültig in der ganzen Schweiz", "Stellenwechsel möglich" or "arbeitsberechtigt": a third-country permit G is valid only in the issuing canton's border zone and every job change needs a new authorisation [C45, C47, C55, C56], and admission depends on the employer's application and the priority test [C48, C50, C51, C52, C53, C54].
6. Keep an "omit" choice: the portals treat nationality and permit as optional [C81] and BIZ Bern treats them as voluntary [C68].
7. Write the German wording with "ss" [C70]; do not copy "Außerdem" from the SEM page [C44].
8. Apply the same rule to the cover letter: no sentence on the permit beyond the option picked.

## Lacune dichiarate

- No institutional source (SEM, ch.ch, the cantons read, SDBB/CSFO, SECO/arbeit.swiss, BIZ Bern, UZH, ETH, Lugano, EURES) says how an applicant who holds no Swiss permit should word the status. They only say to state the permit held. The wording of eu_efta_abroad is a recommendation built on the facts, not a documented convention.
- No Swiss source was found that uses or recommends the tag (EU) after the nationality; it comes from the task's example. The German form of the nationality (country name or adjective) was not checked against a template in this run.
- Only one source addresses the CV wording for the no-permit case: Randstad Italia, a recruiter's Italian site (T2). No Swiss portal (jobs.ch, jobup.ch) gives a wording, and no Randstad Switzerland page was found.
- The wording was not tested with Swiss recruiters: there is no evidence on how 'ohne Schweizer Bewilligung' or '(EU)' is read.
- Ticino: the documents required in the digital procedure that is mandatory since 3 August 2026 (for example a criminal-record extract or self-declaration) could not be read; the form is a JavaScript application, the old form page says it is no longer available and the cantonal FAQ page returns 404. A search snippet mentioning a criminal-record certificate was not verified and is not cited.
- ch.ch: no page dedicated to cross-border commuters could be opened (the guessed address returns 404). The page on working as a foreign national does not describe the permit G. ch.ch pages returned an error page to the fetch tool and were read with a direct request.
- Who files the request differs by canton and no single federal sentence settles it for the permit G EU/EFTA; Art. 11 para. 3 AIG (employer applies) is the general rule of the AIG.
- When a permit G lapses after the job ends: St. Gallen says at the end of the employment, Ticino six months after the activity ceases. The federal rule was not located.
- Third-country cross-border permits: only the SEM, Geneva, Ticino and Basel-Stadt texts were read; the Vaud, Graubünden and St. Gallen pages for third-country nationals were not opened. No cantonal page states explicitly that no quota applies; that follows from Art. 25 para. 2 AIG.
- Liechtenstein nationals (the SEM factsheet mentions a special arrangement) and UK nationals with rights acquired before 2021 were not researched.
- Self-employed cross-border commuters and apprentices were left out.
- The permit fields of employers' online application forms were not checked; the CV line and the answer given in the form must agree.
- French, Italian, German and Austrian public employment services and Infobest were not checked for CV guidance; the EURES-T Oberrhein page was read and gives no guidance on the status line.
- The SEM page on the permit G EU/EFTA was last changed on 01.01.2022 and the third-country page on 09.09.2022; later changes in Swiss-EU law, if any, were not researched beyond the directives of January 2026 and the AIG as of 12 June 2026.
- Quotes were checked against the page text and the text layer of the PDFs; in the PDFs, hyphens at line breaks were removed. The SECO brochure's text layer shows dots for spaces, so that quote was confirmed on the rendered page.
