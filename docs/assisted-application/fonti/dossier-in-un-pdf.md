# Fonti: Un PDF o file separati per la candidatura via e-mail

Pagine lette il 3 ottobre 2026. Ricerca e verifica sono di due agenti distinti: il secondo ha riaperto ogni
fonte e cercato di confutare ogni affermazione. Le citazioni sono nella lingua della fonte; il testo della
ricerca è in inglese, come è stato prodotto. Le **decisioni** prese su queste fonti sono in
[`../decisioni.md`](../decisioni.md): dove la raccomandazione del ricercatore e la decisione differiscono,
vale la decisione.

Domanda: One PDF or separate attachments for a job application sent by e-mail in Switzerland (adults and apprentices; German, French, Italian, English). Read-only web research, pages opened on 2026-10-03. In the quotes, ' | ' marks a line break or list bullet of the original.

## Esito della verifica

- Affermazioni: 111; verdetti: 111 confirmed.
- La raccomandazione regge: no, vedi le obiezioni.

### Obiezioni del verificatore

- Point 2 and packaging.adult.email attribute the single PDF with the letter first to the federal brochure ('This is what the federal brochure says'). The brochure does not say that. In all three editions the one-document sentence follows directly the advice to keep the diplomas, certificates and references ready as PDF because they may be requested soon after the first e-mail (FR 'ces pièces', IT 'questi documenti'). For the first contact it speaks of a CV with photo and possibly a letter, and the letter may instead be the e-mail text. No edition says to merge the letter and the CV, and none gives an order inside a file. C1, C6 and C9 are accurate quotes used for more than they say.
- Direct counter-evidence was missed. The St.Gallen cantonal leaflet LP-2120 (2025, adults) prescribes the cover letter as its own PDF and the CV with certificates and diplomas together as one PDF. The official adult texts that say how letter and CV are packaged for e-mail keep them as separate files (SECO leaflet, Basel-Stadt in both editions, St.Gallen, Zürich Studienwahl) or leave it open (Bern 1-2 PDFs, Graubünden at most three attachments). 'One PDF' is stated by jobs.ch, jobup.ch, Robert Half and yousty.ch, and there it means the whole dossier with certificates, not letter plus CV. C53 concerns uploading, and its French and Italian versions say 'as a PDF file' and 'in PDF format'.
- Point 4 makes 'letter plus CV, no certificates' the default and attaches certificates only when the ad asks for a complete dossier. That rests on one paragraph of the 2018/2019 brochure. The newer SECO page (2026) calls a complete dossier fundamental and lists work certificates and diplomas. The SECO/EURES brochure and the EURES portal tell EU job seekers that a Swiss application generally includes copies of all work certificates and diplomas, and reserve 'letter plus CV only' for unsolicited applications. Zürich, St.Gallen, Bern, Graubünden, jobs.ch and Robert Half say the same for answers to ads. The channel gate allows only answers to ads, which is where this default is weakest; it fits unsolicited applications, which the gate excludes.
- Limits. The 5-page and 2 MB cap comes from the same brochure paragraph. The rule is absent from the 2006 edition and from the 2026 page, and the brochure PDFs were laid out in 2011 (Italian) and 2015 (German) according to their metadata. It cannot hold a dossier that the same sources call complete (arbeit.swiss allows a three-page CV). 'All attachments together 5 MB' cites C73 (apprentices in St.Gallen) and C80 (a limit for the CV file alone) and exceeds the only Ticino-authored figure, 2-3 MB in total, as well as SECO's 1 MB and 2 MB. The apprentice option says 2-3 MB in total while the limits option says 5 MB: the two settings contradict each other.
- The fallback '(1) letter + CV, (2) certificates and diplomas' is not attested in any Swiss source cited or found. The attested splits are: letter | CV with enclosures (St.Gallen); letter | CV | certificates | diplomas (Basel-Stadt, the Geneva job description in C97); letter | CV | one enclosures file (Ticino UOSP, Vaud). 'Never more than three attachments' contradicts the two official texts that prescribe four.
- Order. Only Basel-Stadt states an order rule, and for separate attachments. The other 'letter first' citations are lists of contents, the same kind of evidence the recommendation discounts when the CV comes first (C52, C90). CV-first items that were missed or not weighed: the Ticino UOSP page and its 2025 e-mail model, the Vaud file list (C75), the jobs.ch list. Bern calls the CV the most important document and says a letter is not always asked for, so C36 does not support 'letter first'. SBB and PostFinance made the letter optional, and the Geneva employment office says it is read less and less. In Ticino-authored material the CV is named first at least as often as the letter, so 'letter first' is not established for Italian-speaking Switzerland, and in a merged file it puts the CV on page 2.
- 'Safest default' is not shown. SBB wants the CV as its own document so that the data are stored correctly, and Zürich warns that platforms pre-sort documents automatically. A merged file that opens with a letter is the worst case for that, and no source says it is harmless when an employer feeds e-mail attachments into such a system. Two named PDFs (letter, CV) stay within every numeric limit found in Italian (1 MB, 2 MB, 2-3 MB, three attachments) and match the SECO leaflet and every Ticino-authored example; they depart only from the brochure's 'un solo allegato', whose scope is unclear. The sources therefore do not single out single_pdf as the safe local default that a new Remote Config flag needs under the workspace rules.
- Apprentices (point 6). 'Do not merge' and 'Geneva alone says one PDF' are wrong as stated: yousty.ch, the main apprenticeship portal, tells applicants to save all documents as one single PDF, and its partner tip says the same with a 3 MB limit. The option also contradicts itself: letter + CV + one PDF per kind of enclosure (school reports, stage reports, test results) is five files, above the stated 2 to 4, and filename.separate offers only one enclosure name per language. For Ticino the cantonal rule is three files: CV, letter, and one PDF with all enclosures, 2-3 MB in total.
- Point 10 says the only Ticino-authored rule is C69/C70. Three UOSP sheets created on 19 August 2025 and linked from the cantonal page 'Vorrei fare un tirocinio' were missed. They give a subject ('Candidatura tirocinio – [Nome della professione]'), a model e-mail that names the attachments with the CV first, the file names CV_NomeCognome.pdf and Lettera_NomeCognome.pdf, 2-3 MB in total and a sending window. The Italian apprentice subject and sentence in the options come from the SDBB translation instead; they are usable, but the Ticino model exists and was not considered.
- Group fit, 'tirocinio'. The apprentice options are keyed on 'tirocinio, Lehrstelle, place d'apprentissage'. In Ticino 'tirocinio' is an apprenticeship; for candidates from Italy, the audience of a cross-border site, it is an internship. A candidate or an ad using the word in the Italian sense would get the apprentice packaging (school reports, stage reports, test results) and the pupil-style sentence. The setting has to key on an ad for basic vocational training (AFC/CFP, EFZ/EBA, CFC/AFP). It does not fit adults who retrain either, since they have no recent school reports.
- Group fit, certificates. Candidates trained and employed abroad, such as Italian cross-border workers and third-country nationals, usually hold no Swiss-style work certificates, and their diplomas raise the question of recognition (Lugano: 'per quelli esteri potrebbe essere richiesta l'equipollenza'; Graubünden: 'Per i diplomi esteri, se disponibili, invii anche i rispettivi riconoscimenti'; EURES: state which Swiss title the diploma corresponds to). body.offer_certificates is truthful only if the candidate knows what a 'certificato di lavoro' is; 'the candidate stated they hold them' is not enough without that explanation, and the options say nothing about recognition documents.
- Wording defects. (a) French: the fixed 'de {titre}' is wrong before a vowel or mute h ('poste de assistant', 'apprentissage de employé de commerce'); the official models elide ('au poste d'assistante médicale'). This affects both French subjects and all three French attachment sentences. (b) filename.separate: a file called Zeugnisse, Certificats, Certificati or Certificates that also holds diplomas breaks the option's own condition that each file contains only what its name states. (c) There is no attachment sentence for the adult mode letter_cv_separate; if Remote Config switches the mode, 'in einem PDF' and 'in un unico PDF' become false. (d) English 'Application of {name}' is not idiomatic, and SECO/EURES say the documents must be in a national language, with English only where a multinational accepts it. (e) 'Every naming source uses type plus first name and surname' is not so: the SECO leaflets, the SDBB pages and the Graubünden apprentice sheet show names without the person's name, and Neuchâtel, jobup.ch and USI put the surname first.
- Body. The SECO leaflet (Italian and French), St.Gallen and the SECO brochure warn against standard or mass e-mails, and Bern asks for a personal text and a personal salutation. A fixed body in which only the job title changes, sent for many candidates to the same employers, is recognisable as exactly that. The recommendation does not address this risk.
- Settings an automated sender needs, which the sources state and the recommendation omits: the sender address must be the candidate's own (SECO leaflet: not 'dall'e-mail di un'altra persona'); no generic recipient address such as info@ unless the ad gives it (SECO brochure and leaflet, Bern, Graubünden); sending on working days in office hours (Basel-Stadt 8.00-17.00, Ticino UOSP 8:00-19:00); no read receipt and no formatting in the e-mail (Graubünden, Robert Half); a copy for the candidate, since the Ticino URC guide tells registered job seekers to keep a copy of the applications sent as documentation of their job search.
- Channel gate. The list of employers rests on one FAQ or one ad each (the Coop sentence is about unsolicited applications), is incomplete (PostFinance also refuses e-mail) and will go stale; the rule has to be read from each ad. Point 2 cites C111 for unsolicited applications although the gate allows only ads that give an e-mail address. For cases where the ad does not specify the channel, the SECO leaflet says the application goes by post, and the Ticino UOSP recommends paper or delivery in person.
- What survived, and method. These parts are supported: channel first and obey the ad; PDF only; small files; clear file names; certificates grouped newest first; a short e-mail that names the attachments and ends with the contact details; one e-mail per application; on portals the CV in its own field. No proposed wording promises an outcome or asserts a right; the defects are of grammar, scope and fit. All 111 quotes were found in the cited sources on 2026-10-03 (PDFs read from locally extracted text). Not opened: career.post.ch (HTTP 429), the St.Gallen plain-language guide on hallo.sg.ch and a Solothurn leaflet (HTTP 404), baselland.ch (HTTP 403).

## Affermazioni e fonti

### C1 (T1) — verifica: confirmed

SECO brochure, German edition (form. 711.253d 03.19, the only application brochure currently listed on arbeit.swiss): for an e-mail application, combine all documents in one document of at most 5 pages and 2 MB.

- Fonte: SECO / arbeit.swiss, «Wie bewerbe ich mich richtig?» p. 15 — <https://www.arbeit.swiss/api/media/fileservice/sdweb-docs-prod-arbeitswiss-files/files/2026/07/09/0f28a379-a154-4246-9f3b-f71647124c96.pdf>
- Citazione: «Fassen Sie alle Unterlagen in einem Dokument zusammen. Dieses sollte max. 5 Seiten umfassen und nicht grösser als 2 MB sein. Achten Sie auf die Darstellung der Anhänge. Oft werden elektronische Bewerbungen ausgedruckt.»
- Nota del verificatore: Found on p. 15; imprint 'form. 711.253d 03.19'. Context caveat: the sentence follows 'Halten Sie Ihre Unterlagen in PDF-Form bereit, da diese eventuell schon kurz nach Ihrer ersten E-Mail angefordert werden können', so it can be read as covering the documents sent on request. It does not say to merge letter and CV and gives no order.

- Nota del verificatore: Found on p. 15.

## Raccomandazione del ricercatore

This field is recommendation; what the sources state is in the claims.

1. CHANNEL GATE FIRST. Do not send by e-mail when the ad links a form or portal or says applications are accepted only online: hand the case to the portal flow. EOC, Coop and the Ticino cantonal administration refuse e-mail applications, Migros prefers its portal, SECO says to use only the form when the ad names one (C25, C86, C87, C88, C89, C91, C92). If the ad prescribes the packaging (number of files, one PDF, size), obey the ad and not the defaults below (C37, C97, C110).

2. ADULTS (qualified post or first job), E-MAIL, NO INSTRUCTION IN THE AD, ALL FOUR LANGUAGES: send ONE PDF, cover letter first, CV second. This is what the federal brochure says in German, French and Italian (C1, C6, C9), what berufsberatung.ch says in German (C53) and what jobs.ch, jobup.ch and Robert Half say (C78, C79, C93); for unsolicited applications SECO expects exactly letter plus CV (C111). One small file also respects every cantonal limit found: 1-2 PDFs in Bern (C31, C32), at most three attachments in Graubünden and for Zürich Matura leavers (C41, C45, C48). It departs from Basel-Stadt, which wants four numbered files (C42), and from the SECO leaflet, which shows separately named files (C21, C26); neither forbids merging.

3. ORDER INSIDE THE PDF: cover letter, CV, work certificates newest first, diplomas and training certificates newest first, other documents (C5, C28, C36, C42, C43, C47, C99; newest first also C15, C17, C18, C51). Two Ticino texts name the CV before the letter (C52, C90), but as lists of contents, not as an order rule.

4. CERTIFICATES. Default: letter plus CV only, with no certificates at first contact (C2, C7, C10). Attach certificates only when the candidate supplied them and the ad asks for a complete dossier or for certificates. If everything stays within 5 pages and 2 MB keep one PDF; otherwise send two PDFs, (1) letter plus CV, (2) certificates and diplomas grouped in one file (C15, C17, C18, C34, C70, C74). Never one file per certificate, never more than three attachments, never a ZIP (C45, C48, C83, C84, C85, C110).

5. LIMITS TO ENFORCE: first-contact single PDF at most 5 pages, target 1 MB, hard cap 2 MB (C1, C6, C9, C22, C27); any attachment at most 3 MB (C45, C48, C69, C76, C94); all attachments together at most 5 MB (C73, C80); PDF only; one e-mail per application (C3). The 8-25 MB of mail providers (C33) is a technical ceiling, not the target.

6. APPRENTICESHIP SEEKERS: do not merge. Letter and CV as two named PDFs plus one PDF per kind of enclosure (school reports newest first, stage reports, test results), 2 to 4 attachments, 2-3 MB in total (C56, C57, C58, C62, C65, C68, C69, C70, C72, C74, C75, C77). Geneva alone says one PDF if possible (C76).

7. PORTAL UPLOADS: the CV as its own PDF in the CV field, everything else merged in one file, as the form asks (C82, C83, C84, C85, C95).

8. FILE NAMES: language of the ad (C104); pattern document type plus first name and surname, which every naming source uses in some form (C34, C44, C45, C48, C70, C75, C77, C81, C96). Values in options filename.single and filename.separate. Underscores and ASCII are my technical precaution, not a rule from the sources, which also show spaces and accents.

9. SUBJECT AND E-MAIL BODY: subject with the job title copied from the ad plus the applicant's name (C3, C8, C12, C24, C35, C40, C49, C100, C103). Body of 2 to 5 sentences that does not repeat the letter (C4, C13, C23, C35, C102), says what is attached (C39, C46, C50, C61, C64, C67, C69, C107, C108) and ends with the contact details. The attachment sentence may name only what is really attached, and certificates on request may be offered only if the candidate said they hold them (C11, C109). Wordings in options body.*.

10. STRENGTH OF THE EVIDENCE FOR ITALIAN-SPEAKING SWITZERLAND: WEAK for one PDF as such. The Italian T1 texts with an explicit adult rule disagree: SECO brochure 2018, one attachment, 5 pages, 2 MB (C9); SECO leaflet 2018, offered next to it by the Ticino Sezione del lavoro, separately named PDFs, 8-10 pages, 1 MB (C21, C22, C29); arbeit.swiss page 2026, certificates grouped in one PDF and files in the plural (C18, C19); Graubünden, at most 3 attachments of 2-3 MB (C48). All are translations of German texts. Ticino offices add no adult rule of their own (C29, C30, C52, C55); the only Ticino-authored rule is for apprentices (C69, C70). No Ticino portal, recruiter or employer text on packaging was found, and the large Ticino employers checked take no e-mail at all (C87, C89, C92). What is well supported in Italian too: PDF only, clear file names carrying the person's name, small files, certificates grouped newest first, a short e-mail that names the attachments, and obeying the ad's channel. A 2-3 page single PDF under 1 MB breaks none of the limits stated in any Italian-language source, which makes it the safest default; it is not shown to be what Ticino employers prefer. For that reason keep the adult packaging mode (single_pdf or letter_cv_separate) per language as a Firebase Remote Config value with single_pdf as the local default, so it can be changed without a release.

## Lacune dichiarate

- SECO leaflet 716.208 in German («Elektronische Bewerbung»): not found and not opened. Only the Italian edition (hosted by Cantone Ticino) and the French edition (hosted by Canton Jura) were read, so the German wording of the separate-files rule is unverified.
- SECO does not reconcile its own texts. The brochure (2018/2019: one document, 5 pages, 2 MB, letter and CV only at first contact), the leaflet (2018: separately named files, 8-10 pages, 1 MB) and the web page (2026: complete dossier, certificates grouped in one PDF, no limits) differ, and no SECO text says which prevails. No source explains where the 2 MB or 1 MB figures come from; both date from 2018.
- No source in any language gives a file name for a merged letter-plus-CV PDF, and none states the order of the parts inside a merged file as a rule; the order is inferred from how the dossier contents are listed. The file names proposed in the options are built by analogy with the attested pattern (document type plus name).
- Italian-speaking Switzerland, adults: no Ticino-authored rule on one file versus several was found. The Sezione del lavoro and the URC only relay SECO, the Città di Lugano and the Città dei mestieri della Svizzera italiana pages say nothing on files, and the Italian adult page of orientamento.ch omits the 'right order, if possible one PDF' sentence of the German page.
- No T2 source from Ticino (job portals such as tuttojob.ch, carriera.ch, e-lavoro.ch, or recruiters) with guidance on packaging was found, and jobs.ch has no Italian edition of its guides. No survey or other empirical data on what Swiss or Ticino recruiters prefer was found; every source is a prescriptive guide.
- Vaud adults (OCOSP guide of February 2023), Geneva employment office and Kanton Zürich adult page: none says whether to send one file or several by e-mail. Stadt Zürich Laufbahnzentrum: no text on the point found.
- Swiss Post: career.post.ch answered HTTP 429 on every attempt and the older «fit im Beruf» PDF redirects to post.ch, so nothing from Swiss Post is cited.
- Coop: the FAQ sentences were obtained through the fetch tool only (direct download refused with HTTP 403), so they could not be checked against the raw page; the FAQ states no file format, size or count. Migros: the FAQ states no file rules. EOC: only the ad text and the careers page were read, not the upload form itself (fields, accepted formats, size limit).
- English: no Swiss source gives an English subject line, e-mail sentence or dossier file name; only USI's CV file-name pattern is attested. The English wordings in the options are translations.
- No source says whether a merged PDF harms automatic parsing when an employer imports e-mail attachments into an ATS. The SBB rule (CV as its own document) concerns portal upload only.
- Method limits: PDF text was extracted locally from the files the fetch tool downloaded; the German SECO brochure encodes spaces as dots in its text layer, so its quotes were re-typed from the extraction and hyphenation at line ends was removed. Quote checks compared letters and digits only, so punctuation or spacing inside a quote may differ slightly from the source.
- The prior study's SECO URL (upload path 2026/06/03) still opens with the same German text; arbeit.swiss now links a re-upload dated 2026/07/09 for each language, which is what is cited here.
